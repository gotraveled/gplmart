# Etsy & Dropshipping Business Toolkit

Every number your Etsy or dropshipping shop runs on - fees, profit, orders and listings - in one workbook with live formulas.

## What's inside

- **Etsy-Dropshipping-Business-Toolkit.xlsx** - the workbook:
  - *Start Here* - tab guide
  - *Etsy Fee Calculator* - real Etsy fee math (6.5% transaction + ~3% payment + optional 12% offsite ads) - net profit per sale, margin and break-even price
  - *Profit Tracker* - log every sale (revenue, COGS, fees, ads, shipping) with running profit totals
  - *Order Log* - every order with status dropdown (Paid / Shipped / Delivered / Refunded / Issue)
  - *Listing Tracker* - views, favorites, sales, conversion rate and revenue per listing

## How to use

1. Unzip and open the `.xlsx` in Excel, Google Sheets (File > Import) or Numbers.
2. Yellow cells are inputs - everything else calculates.
3. Price every new listing through the Fee Calculator first; log sales weekly in Profit Tracker; review Listing Tracker every Sunday to kill under-1% converters.

## Notes

- Etsy fees are current as of 2026 (6.5% transaction, ~3% + $0.25 payment, 12%/15% offsite ads). If Etsy changes rates, update the percentage cells on the Fee Calculator tab.
- The toolkit works for Shopify/dropshipping too - the fee tab is Etsy-specific, the trackers are universal.
