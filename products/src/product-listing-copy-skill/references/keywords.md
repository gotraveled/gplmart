# Keyword research for listings

## The method (free, 30 min/product)

1. **Amazon/Flipkart search bar**: type product type, note autocomplete suggestions - these are REAL buyer searches
2. **Competitor listings**: top 3 competitors' titles + bullets - what keywords repeat?
3. **Google Trends**: rising vs declining terms in your category
4. **PAA (People Also Ask)**: questions buyers ask = FAQ content + long-tail keywords

## Keyword types

- **Primary**: exact product term ("steel lunch box") - in title + bullets
- **Secondary**: use case modifiers ("office tiffin", "school lunch") - in description
- **Long-tail**: specific queries ("leak proof steel lunch box for office") - in FAQ/description
- **Regional**: Hindi/regional terms for Indian marketplaces ("tiffin dabba", "roti box")

## Backend keywords (invisible fields)

Amazon backend: 250 bytes, comma-separated, no repeats of title words  
Shopify: tags + SEO title/description fields  
Flipkart: auto-indexed from title/description - write naturally

## What NOT to do

- No competitor brand names (trademark violation)
- No irrelevant popular keywords ("mobile phone" on a lunch box = spam flag)
- No keyword stuffing - readability first, density ~1-2%
