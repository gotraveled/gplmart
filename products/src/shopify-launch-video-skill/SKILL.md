# Store launch films - announce it like a drop

A launch video has one job: make the viewer feel the store is NEW and worth checking NOW. It's a promo
with urgency built in - launch date, offer, first-look products, and a hard CTA.

No presenter, no voiceover. If someone asks for those, say this skill does not do it.

Brand-agnostic: asks for store name, logo, product photos, launch offer and launch date fresh every run.

**Higgsfield first, fal.ai fallback** - same provider rules as the sibling video skills.

---

## The launch arc (~15-25s)

1. **Tease (0-2s)** - darkness/blur resolves into the hero product or "COMING SOON" energy
2. **First look (2-7s)** - 3-4 hero products in fast succession, hard cuts
3. **The drop (7-13s)** - launch offer big on screen: discount code, bundle, free shipping
4. **Why now (13-18s)** - one proof beat: bestsellers, reviews count, or "just landed" energy
5. **CTA close (18-25s)** - logo + URL + "Shop the launch" end card

## Intake - one batch

1. Store name + launch date (or "live now")
2. Logo file (PNG/SVG transparent)
3. 4-8 product photos (the launch collection)
4. The offer, verbatim: discount code, % off, free shipping terms
5. Launch hook: what's NEW - new collection, new store, restock, seasonal drop
6. Duration + aspect ratio (9:16 default)
7. Mood: hype-drop / premium / playful / minimal-luxury

## Launch-specific rules

- The offer text is the biggest type in the film - it must be readable on a phone in 1 second
- Date/urgency only if real: "Launch week only" needs an actual end date, else say "now live"
- Never fake scarcity ("only 3 left") unless the store can actually back it
- End card ALWAYS carries the URL - a launch video without a destination is wasted spend
- Countdown timers in generated footage look fake - do urgency with text cards, not animated counters

## Pipeline

Same as sibling skills: spec → shot plan → cost confirm → generate → Remotion finish (logo end card,
fade, optional audio) → review checklist.
