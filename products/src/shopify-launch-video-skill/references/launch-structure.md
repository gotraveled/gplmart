# Launch structure - beats, timing, text rules

## Beat sheet (20s reference)

| Beat | Time | Content | Text card |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tease | 0-2s | Product resolves from dark/blur | "something new." |
| First look | 2-7s | 3-4 products, hard cuts ~1.2s each | product names only if needed |
| The drop | 7-13s | Hero product slow push + offer | "LAUNCH WEEK - 20% OFF" |
| Why now | 13-18s | Bestsellers/reviews/lifestyle | "just landed" or social proof |
| CTA | 18-20s+ | Logo + URL end card | "Shop the launch → gplmart.com" |

Scale beats proportionally for 15s or 25s - never cram 5 beats into 10s.

## Text card rules

- Max 6 words per card, max 3 cards per film
- The offer card gets the most screen time and biggest type
- Font: bold, high contrast, never thinner than the brand's heading weight
- Cards hold 1.5-2.5s minimum - faster is unreadable on mobile

## Urgency that doesn't lie

- Real end date → "Launch week only" or "Until Sunday"
- No end date → "Now live" - never invent deadlines
- Stock counts → only if the store tracks real inventory
- Fake countdown animations → banned; text cards carry urgency

## Shot variety

- 4-6 distinct shots minimum for 20s
- Mix: hero push-in, orbit (max 1), top-down flat lay, macro texture, fast cuts
- Every cut should feel intentional - if two adjacent shots look the same, merge or reshoot
