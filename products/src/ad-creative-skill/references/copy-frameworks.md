# Copy frameworks + examples

## Hook formulas (first line - most important)

- **Pain callout**: "Still [doing painful thing] the hard way?"
- **Relatable moment**: "POV: you finally found [solution]"
- **Bold claim**: "This [product] replaced my [expensive alternative]"
- **Question**: "What if [desired outcome] took 10 minutes?"
- **Pattern interrupt**: "Nobody tells you [surprising truth about category]"
- **Social proof**: "[Number] people bought this last week - here's why"
- **Curiosity gap**: "I didn't believe it until I tried it..."

## Headline formulas (<40 chars)

- "[Benefit] in [timeframe]"
- "The [category] everyone is switching to"
- "[Number]% off - this week only"
- "Finally, a [product] that actually [works]"
- "Stop [pain]. Start [benefit]."

## Primary text structure (feed ads)

```
[Hook line - stops scroll]
[2-3 sentences: problem → solution → proof]
[Offer/CTA line]
[Emoji optional, max 1-2]
```

Keep under 125 chars for full visibility without "see more" - the expansion kills CTR.

## CTA button selection

- "Shop Now" - default for purchase intent
- "Get Offer" - when discount exists
- "Learn More" - for high-consideration products
- "Sign Up" - lead gen only
- "Send Message" - WhatsApp/chat commerce (India)
