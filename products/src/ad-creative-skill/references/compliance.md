# Compliance - claims rules by category

Breaking ad platform rules = rejected ads, disabled accounts. Know the category rules.

## Health / supplements / beauty

- NO medical claims: "cures", "treats", "FDA approved" (unless literally true + documented)
- "Supports", "promotes", "helps maintain" = safer wording
- Before/after images: restricted on Meta - use with extreme care or avoid
- Ingredient claims must be verifiable

## Finance / earning claims

- "Make $X/month" = instant rejection territory
- Use "potential", "results vary", earnings disclaimers
- No guaranteed returns language ever

## Kids / baby products

- COPPA-sensitive - no data collection implications in creative
- Safety claims need documentation
- Extra scrutiny on toy safety language

## Weight loss / fitness

- "Lose X kg in Y days" = banned on most platforms
- Focus on habit/lifestyle, not numbers
- Before/after heavily restricted

## Universal rules

- No fake countdowns ("offer ends tonight" when it doesn't)
- No fake scarcity ("only 3 left" when untrue)
- No misleading "was/now" pricing (the "was" price must be real, recent)
- No shocking/borderline imagery - account quality score matters more than one viral ad
- Alcohol, gambling, crypto, political = restricted categories, know the rules first

## When unsure

Write the claim conservatively. A rejected ad costs more than a weaker claim that runs.
