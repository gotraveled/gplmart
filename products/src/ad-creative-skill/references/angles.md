# The three angles deep-dive

## Angle 1 - Problem-solution

**Who it targets**: cold traffic who has the problem but doesn't know your product exists.

**Structure**: name the pain → agitate slightly → introduce product as the fix → proof.

**Example (kitchen gadget)**: "Tired of chopping onions and crying? This chopper does it in 30 seconds - no tears, no fuss. 10,000+ home cooks switched."

**Best for**: products solving a clear, relatable pain. Weak for novelty/desire products.

## Angle 2 - Curiosity/desire

**Who it targets**: broad audiences, impulse buys, visually interesting products.

**Structure**: create open loop → partial reveal → payoff.

**Example (phone accessory)**: "This $15 gadget does what $200 flagships can't... wait for the magnet test."

**Best for**: unique/novel products, gadgets, anything with a "wait, what?" factor. Risk: curiosity without payoff = bounce.

## Angle 3 - Offer/proof

**Who it targets**: warm audiences (retargeting), price-sensitive buyers, launch campaigns.

**Structure**: lead with offer or proof number → product → urgency.

**Example**: "Launch week: 25% off our bestselling organizer. Free shipping over ₹999. Code: NEW25."

**Best for**: retargeting, sales, restocks. Weak for cold traffic (they don't know you yet).

## Mixing rule

Never run two angles with the same hook style - they cannibalize each other's data.
