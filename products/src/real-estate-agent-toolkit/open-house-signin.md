# Open House Sign-In Sheet — Printable Template

Fill this template to generate a clean, professional sign-in sheet for every open house. For best results, print on US Letter (8.5" × 11"), portrait orientation, single-sided.

<!--
PRINTING INSTRUCTIONS
---------------------
1. Copy this markdown file (or open in any markdown previewer).
2. Print to PDF or directly to paper using US Letter, portrait, 100% scale (disable "fit to page").
3. Print on 20–24 lb paper. For a premium feel, use 24 lb résumé or cover stock.
4. Print one side only — the back stays blank for additional notes or to look cleaner on the sign-in table.
5. Place on a clipboard or sign-in board at the entry. Keep 10–15 copies; bring a pen and a print-out of your listing flyer.
6. Optional: laminate a master version with dry-erase ink if your MLS/state allows, or reprint fresh copies per open house to look professional.
7. Store completed sheets in the listing file; key leads within 24 hours (see lead-followup.md for the cadence).
-->
