# Client Onboarding Packet — New Client Welcome & Setup

A complete onboarding system for new seller and buyer clients. Use this packet to set expectations, collect documents, and establish communication preferences before the transaction begins.

> **How to use:** Send the welcome letter within 24 hours of signing the listing/buyer agreement. Attach the document checklist and communication preferences form. Review the "what to expect" guide together on your first call.

---

## 1. Welcome Letter Template

**Subject:** Welcome to [Your Name / Brokerage] — Let's Get Started!

---

Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for choosing [Your Name / Brokerage] to help you [sell your home / find your next home]. I'm excited to work with you and I'm committed to making this process as smooth and successful as possible.

**Here's what happens next:**

1. **Within 24 hours:** I'll send you a document checklist and a communication preferences form.
2. **Within 48 hours:** We'll schedule our first working session to review your timeline, goals, and next steps.
3. **Within 72 hours:** I'll have your [listing live / search criteria set] and we'll begin the real work.

**My promise to you:**

- **Communication:** You'll hear from me at least [twice weekly] with updates, and I'll respond to your calls/texts/emails within [24 hours].
- **Transparency:** I'll show you the data behind every decision — pricing, marketing, negotiations.
- **Advocacy:** I'm on your side. My job is to get you the best outcome, not just close a deal.

**What I need from you:**

- Please complete the document checklist I'm sending separately.
- Fill out the communication preferences form so I know how you like to be contacted.
- Be honest with me about your timeline, budget, and concerns — I'll do the same for you.

I'm looking forward to a successful partnership. If you have any questions before our first call, don't hesitate to reach out.

Warm regards,

[Your Name]
[Brokerage Name]
[Phone] | [Email] | [Website]

## 5. "What to Expect" Guide for Sellers

### 5.1 Before Listing

- **Preparation:** We'll declutter, deep clean, and make minor repairs. Professional photos and possibly staging will be scheduled.
- **Disclosures:** You'll complete required disclosures (lead paint, property condition, etc.). Be honest — buyers can sue for non-disclosure.
- **Pricing:** We'll review comps and market data to set a competitive price.

### 5.2 During Marketing

- **Showings:** Buyers will schedule through the MLS or their agent. You can choose to be present or absent.
- **Open houses:** We'll host 2–4 open houses in the first two weekends.
- **Feedback:** We'll share showing feedback weekly. If there's no activity, we may adjust price or marketing.

### 5.3 During Negotiations

- **Offers:** You'll review all offers with me. We'll analyze terms, not just price.
- **Counteroffers:** We'll negotiate to get you the best overall package.
- **Contingencies:** We'll ensure contingencies protect you (financing, inspection, appraisal).

### 5.4 During Escrow

- **Inspections:** Buyers will inspect the property. We'll negotiate repairs or credits.
- **Appraisal:** The lender will order an appraisal. If it comes in low, we'll negotiate or challenge.
- **Final walkthrough:** Buyers will do a final walkthrough before closing.

### 5.5 At Closing

- **Signing:** You'll sign documents at the title company or via mobile notary.
- **Funds:** Proceeds will be wired or mailed to you.
- **Keys:** You'll hand over keys and any access devices.

### 5.6 After Closing

- **Tax documents:** You'll receive a 1099-S for tax purposes.
- **Moving:** We'll help coordinate your move if needed.
- **Referral:** We'll ask for a testimonial or referral if you're happy with our service.

## 6. "What to Expect" Guide for Buyers

### 6.1 Before Search

- **Pre-approval:** Get pre-approved before searching. This shows sellers you're serious.
- **Search criteria:** We'll define your must-haves, nice-to-haves, and deal-breakers.
- **Market analysis:** We'll review comps and market trends to set realistic expectations.

### 6.2 During Search

- **Showings:** We'll schedule showings for homes that match your criteria.
- **Feedback:** We'll share our thoughts on each home and help you narrow down.
- **Market conditions:** We'll discuss whether it's a buyer's or seller's market.

### 6.3 During Offer

- **Offer strategy:** We'll discuss offer price, contingencies, and terms.
- **Negotiation:** We'll negotiate to get you the best deal.
- **Contingencies:** We'll ensure contingencies protect you (financing, inspection, appraisal).

### 6.4 During Escrow

- **Inspections:** We'll schedule inspections and negotiate repairs or credits.
- **Appraisal:** The lender will order an appraisal. If it comes in low, we'll negotiate or challenge.
- **Final walkthrough:** We'll do a final walkthrough before closing.

### 6.5 At Closing

- **Signing:** You'll sign documents at the title company or via mobile notary.
- **Funds:** You'll wire or bring a cashier's check for closing costs.
- **Keys:** You'll get keys to your new home.

### 6.6 After Closing

- **Moving:** We'll help coordinate your move if needed.
- **Utilities:** We'll remind you to transfer utilities.
- **Referral:** We'll ask for a testimonial or referral if you're happy with our service.
