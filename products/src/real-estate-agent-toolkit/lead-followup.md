# Lead Follow-Up System — Cadence, Scripts & Sphere-of-Influence

A complete follow-up system for converting leads into clients. Consistency beats intensity — the agents who win are the ones who follow up systematically, not the ones who follow up hardest.

> **Core principle:** 80% of sales happen after 5+ follow-ups. Most agents quit after 2. Don't be most agents.

---

## 1. The 5-3-1 Follow-Up Cadence

A proven cadence for new leads (inquiries, open house sign-ins, website leads, referrals).

| Day | Touchpoint | Channel | Purpose |
|-----|------------|---------|---------|
| 0 | Immediate response | Text + email | Acknowledge inquiry, offer value |
| 1 | Follow-up call | Phone | Qualify lead, answer questions |
| 3 | Value touch | Email | Market update, neighborhood info, or relevant content |
| 5 | Check-in text | Text | "Still interested? Any questions?" |
| 7 | Second call attempt | Phone | If no answer, leave voicemail |
| 10 | Value touch #2 | Email | New listing alert, market data, or helpful resource |
| 14 | Personal note | Handwritten | "Thinking of you — here's a market update" |
| 21 | Re-engagement call | Phone | "How can I help? Still looking?" |
| 30 | Final attempt | Text + email | "I'll check back in 30 days unless you tell me otherwise" |
| 60 | Nurture campaign | Email drip | Monthly market updates, still in database |

> **KEY INSIGHT:** The 5-3-1 cadence isn't about pestering — it's about being top-of-mind when the lead is ready. Most buyers/sellers don't transact in the first week; they transact when their timeline aligns with your consistent presence.

## 2. The 10 Touchpoint Message Templates

### Touchpoint 1: Immediate Response (Day 0)

**Text message:**

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. Thanks for reaching out about [property/market]. I'd love to help you find your next home / sell your current one. When's a good time to chat? — [Phone]"

**Email:**

> **Subject:** Thanks for reaching out — let's find your perfect home
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for contacting me about [specific inquiry]. I'm [Your Name] with [Brokerage], and I specialize in [neighborhood/price range/type].
>
> I'd love to learn more about what you're looking for. Are you:
> - Looking to buy?
> - Looking to sell?
> - Just exploring the market?
>
> Reply to this email or call me at [Phone] — I'm happy to help.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

### Touchpoint 2: Follow-Up Call Script (Day 1)

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I saw you reached out about [property/market] — I wanted to follow up and see if you have any questions. Are you still looking to buy/sell, or has your timeline changed? I'm here to help whenever you're ready."

### Touchpoint 3: Value Touch Email (Day 3)

> **Subject:** [Neighborhood] market update — here's what's happening
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I wanted to share a quick update on the [neighborhood/price range] market:
>
> - **Median sale price:** $[X] (up/down [X]% from last month)
> - **Average days on market:** [X] days
> - **Inventory:** [X] active listings
>
> If you're thinking about buying or selling in the next [X] months, I'd love to chat about what this means for you. No pressure — just information.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

### Touchpoint 4: Check-In Text (Day 5)

> "Hi [Name], just checking in — are you still interested in [neighborhood/property]? I've got some new listings that might interest you. Want me to send them over?"

### Touchpoint 5: Second Call Attempt (Day 7)

## 3. Sphere-of-Influence Scripts

Your sphere of influence (SOI) — past clients, friends, family, colleagues — is your most valuable lead source. Here's how to nurture it systematically.

### 3.1 Monthly Market Update Email (SOI)

> **Subject:** [Month] market update — [Neighborhood] homes selling fast
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Here's a quick update on the [neighborhood] market:
>
> - **Median sale price:** $[X] (up/down [X]% from last month)
> - **Average days on market:** [X] days
> - **New listings:** [X] homes
>
> If you know anyone who's thinking about buying or selling, I'd love to help. Just forward this email or give them my number.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

### 3.2 Birthday/Anniversary Text

> "Hi [Name], happy birthday! Hope you're having a great day. If you know anyone who's thinking about buying or selling, I'd love to help. Just let me know — I'm here for you. — [Your Name]"

### 3.3 Just-Listed Announcement

> "Hi [Name], just listed a new home at [Address] — [beds] bed / [baths] bath / $[X]. If you know anyone who's looking, I'd love to help. Want me to send you the details?"

### 3.4 Just-Sold Announcement

> "Hi [Name], just sold a home at [Address] for $[X] — [X] over asking! If you know anyone who's thinking about selling, I'd love to help them get top dollar. Want me to send you the details?"

### 3.5 Referral Request Script

> "Hi [Name], I'm so glad I could help you [buy/sell] your home. If you know anyone who's thinking about buying or selling, I'd love to help them too. Just forward this email or give them my number. Thanks for your trust!"

## 4. Expired/Cancelled Listing Scripts

Expired and cancelled listings are goldmines — the seller tried to sell, it didn't work, and they're frustrated. Here's how to approach them.

### 4.1 Expired Listing Outreach (Day 1 after expiry)

**Text:**

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I saw your home at [Address] just expired off the market. I'm sorry it didn't sell — I know that's frustrating. I'd love to help you get it sold. Are you still interested? When's a good time to chat?"

**Call script:**

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I saw your home at [Address] just expired. I'm sorry it didn't sell — I know that's frustrating. I'd love to help you get it sold. Can I ask what went wrong? Was it price, marketing, or timing? I have some ideas that might help."

### 4.2 Expired Listing Follow-Up (Day 7)

**Text:**

> "Hi [Name], just checking in — are you still interested in selling [Address]? I have some ideas that might help. Want to chat?"

**Call script:**

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I wanted to follow up on [Address]. I know the last listing didn't work out, but I have some ideas that might help — maybe a different price strategy, better marketing, or timing. Are you open to a quick conversation?"

### 4.3 Cancelled Listing Outreach

**Text:**

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I saw you cancelled the listing on [Address]. I'm sorry it didn't work out — I know that's frustrating. If you're still interested in selling, I'd love to help. Are you open to a chat?"

**Call script:**

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I saw you cancelled the listing on [Address]. I'm sorry it didn't work out. If you're still interested in selling, I'd love to help — I have some ideas that might make the difference. Are you open to a quick conversation?"

### 4.4 FSBO Outreach (For Sale By Owner)

**Text:**

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I saw you're selling [Address] on your own — that's great! I'd love to help you get top dollar. Are you open to a quick chat about how I can help?"

**Call script:**

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I saw you're selling [Address] on your own — that's great! I'd love to help you get top dollar. Can I ask how long you've been on the market? Have you had any offers? I have some ideas that might help — better marketing, pricing strategy, or negotiation. Are you open to a quick conversation?"

## 5. Lead Qualification Questions

Before diving into follow-up, qualify the lead to prioritize your time.

### 5.1 For Buyers

1. Are you pre-approved or pre-qualified?
2. What's your timeline? (immediate, 1–3 months, 3–6 months, just looking)
3. What's your budget range?
4. What areas/neighborhoods are you interested in?
5. What type of home? (single-family, condo, townhome)
6. What's most important to you? (price, location, size, condition)

### 5.2 For Sellers

1. Why are you selling? (relocation, upsizing, downsizing, life event)
2. What's your timeline? (immediate, 1–3 months, 3–6 months, just exploring)
3. Have you listed before? What worked/didn't work?
4. What's your ideal price? What's your minimum?
5. Are there any repairs or items you know need attention?
6. Are you open to staging or minor repairs?
