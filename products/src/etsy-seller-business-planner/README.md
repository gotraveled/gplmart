# Etsy Seller Business Planner

Treat your Etsy shop like the business it is: dashboard, listings, content, reviews, fees and goals in one workbook.

## What's inside
- **Etsy-Seller-Business-Planner.xlsx** - 6 tabs:
  - *Shop Dashboard* - monthly revenue, fees, COGS, ads and net margin
  - *Listing Pipeline* - views, favorites, sales, conversion rate per listing
  - *Content Calendar* - weekly Pinterest/Instagram/SEO plan + monthly checklist
  - *Review Tracker* - every review with response status and issue flags
  - *Fee & Profit Log* - per-order Etsy fee math
  - *Goals* - monthly revenue/orders/listings targets vs actual

## How to use
Open in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. Pull monthly numbers from Etsy Stats > Monthly. Review the listing tracker weekly - kill sub-0.5% converters.
