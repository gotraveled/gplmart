# Product fidelity - the rule that matters most

Generated product photos fail in predictable ways. Check every output for:

## The drift checklist

- [ ] Shape/proportions match the real product
- [ ] Brand colours exact (not "close enough")
- [ ] Label text correct - or acceptably blurred, NEVER wrong words
- [ ] No extra/missing components (lid, strap, button, ingredient)
- [ ] Material looks real (fabric weave, glass reflection, food texture)
- [ ] No impossible physics (floating parts, melted packaging, warped labels)

## When drift happens

1. Regenerate with a MORE specific prompt (name the exact label text, describe the distinctive feature)
2. Try a different angle that avoids the problem area
3. Crop/compose so the drifting region is out of frame
4. Last resort: use the real photo for that shot - a real photo beats a fake one always

## The honest line

This skill photographs the REAL product. If asked to "improve" the product itself (make chips look
glossier, make fabric look thicker, remove a defect), decline - that's false advertising territory,
and marketplaces (Amazon especially) ban it.
