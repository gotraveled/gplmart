# Platform image rules

## Amazon
- Main image: PURE WHITE background (RGB 255,255,255), product fills 85%+ of frame, NO props, NO text, NO logos-in-image, NO watermarks
- Max 10 images per listing; secondary images can have lifestyle/context
- Minimum 1000px (recommend 2000px+ for zoom)
- Violations = suppressed listing

## Flipkart
- White or very light background for main image
- Product should fill frame; minimal props allowed on secondary
- Text overlays allowed on secondary images only

## Meesho / GlowRoad
- More permissive - lifestyle images with models/context do well
- Price/offer text overlays common and accepted on these platforms
- Vertical 3:4 or square crops perform best

## Instagram / Facebook ads
- 1:1 or 4:5 feed, 9:16 stories/reels
- Lifestyle > sterile white for social - context sells
- Text overlay space: leave clean areas for ad copy

## Shopify (own store)
- Consistent aspect ratio across catalogue (pick one: square or 4:5)
- Same background treatment for all products = brand cohesion
- 2048px max; Shopify CDN handles sizing
