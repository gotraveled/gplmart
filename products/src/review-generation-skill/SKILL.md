# Review generation - turn buyers into your best salespeople

Products with 50+ reviews convert 3-5x better than products with 5 reviews. This skill builds
the SYSTEM that generates reviews consistently - not fake reviews, but genuine ones from real
customers who actually bought.

---

## Intake - one batch

1. Store/product + average order value
2. Current review count + average rating (if any)
3. Platform: Shopify / Amazon / Flipkart / own site
4. Current review collection: none / email requests / WhatsApp / app
5. Product type: consumable / durable / fashion / electronics
6. Any incentives offered: discount, loyalty points, free gift
7. Pain point: not enough reviews / low ratings / bad review management

## Why reviews matter (the data)

- **Conversion lift**: products with 50+ reviews convert 3-5x better
- **SEO benefit**: review schema = rich snippets in Google = higher CTR
- **Trust signal**: 90% of buyers read reviews before purchasing
- **Product insight**: reviews tell you what's working and what's not
- **Social proof**: UGC photos/videos from reviews = free content

## The review generation system

### Step 1: Make reviewing easy

- **Post-purchase email**: send 7-10 days after delivery (product has been used)
- **WhatsApp request**: for Indian customers, WhatsApp gets 3-5x more responses than email
- **QR code on packaging**: scan → review page (impulse reviews at unboxing)
- **Review link in order confirmation**: easy access, but too early (product not used yet)
- **In-app prompts**: Shopify review apps (Judge.me, Loox, Okendo)

### Step 2: Timing matters

| Product type | Best timing | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Consumables (food, beauty) | 7-14 days after delivery | Enough time to use, still fresh |
| Fashion/apparel | 3-7 days after delivery | Fit assessment done, still excited |
| Electronics/durables | 14-21 days after delivery | Used enough to form opinion |
| Home/kitchen | 7-10 days after delivery | Used in daily routine |

Too early = generic "arrived on time" reviews. Too late = forgotten, low response rate.

### Step 3: The request sequence

**Email/WhatsApp 1** (day 7-10):
```
Hi [Name], hope you're enjoying your [product]! 

Mind sharing a quick review? It helps other customers decide + earns you [incentive].

[Review link]

Thanks! - [Store name]
```

**Follow-up** (day 14, only if no review):
```
Hi [Name], just a quick reminder - would love your feedback on [product].

Even a sentence helps other buyers. [Review link]

No worries if not - thanks anyway!
```

**Final attempt** (day 21):
```
Last ask, [Name]! If you have 30 seconds, a review would mean a lot.

[Review link]

Either way, thanks for being a customer!
```

### Step 4: Incentives (optional but effective)

| Incentive | Cost | Effectiveness | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discount code | 5-10% off next order | High | Low - still genuine reviews |
| Loyalty points | Points toward next purchase | Medium | Low |
| Free gift | Small accessory | Medium | Low |
| Entry into draw | Chance to win product | Medium | Low |
| No incentive | Free | Lower response | Zero risk |

**Important**: incentives should reward the REVIEW, not the RATING. Never offer incentives only for 5-star reviews - that's review manipulation (illegal on Amazon, unethical everywhere).

## Review platform optimization

### Shopify
- **Judge.me**: free plan, unlimited reviews, photo reviews, Q&A
- **Loox**: photo/video reviews, Instagram integration
- **Okendo**: reviews + surveys + loyalty in one
- **Stamped.io**: reviews + loyalty + referrals

### Marketplaces
- **Amazon**: Vine program (early reviewer), Request a Review button (automated)
- **Flipkart**: seller can request reviews post-delivery
- **Meesho**: reviews built into product pages, reseller-focused

## Review management (responding to reviews)

### Positive reviews
- Thank them publicly (shows you care)
- Address specific points they mentioned
- Invite them back ("come back for [new product]")

### Negative reviews
- Respond within 24-48 hours
- Acknowledge the issue, apologize, offer solution
- Take conversation offline if needed (DM/email)
- Never argue publicly - future customers are watching

### Fake review detection
- Watch for: generic text, no purchase verification, multiple reviews same IP/device
- Report suspicious reviews to platform
- Never buy fake reviews - platforms detect and penalize, and it's illegal in many countries

## Review content strategy

### What to ask for
- Specific questions: "How was the fit?", "Does it keep food hot?", "Would you recommend?"
- Photo/video reviews: "Share a photo of [product] in use"
- Detailed reviews: "What did you like most? What could be better?"

### What NOT to ask for
- "Please leave a 5-star review" (review manipulation)
- Incentivized reviews that require positive rating
- Reviews for products not yet received/used
