# Incentive strategies - what works, what's risky

## Incentive types

### Discount code (most effective)
- "Get 10% off your next order for reviewing"
- Cost: only paid if they buy again (not guaranteed)
- Effectiveness: high response rate
- Risk: low - still genuine reviews

### Loyalty points
- "Earn 50 points for your review"
- Cost: points liability (low if points expire)
- Effectiveness: medium
- Risk: low

### Free gift
- "Get free [small accessory] for reviewing"
- Cost: product cost (small if accessory is cheap)
- Effectiveness: medium-high
- Risk: low

### Entry into draw
- "Enter to win [product] - review to enter"
- Cost: one product giveaway
- Effectiveness: medium
- Risk: low

### No incentive
- Just ask nicely
- Cost: zero
- Effectiveness: lower response rate (20-30% vs 40-60% with incentive)
- Risk: zero

## Incentive rules (critical)

### DO
- Reward the REVIEW, not the RATING
- Offer incentive regardless of star rating ("review and get X")
- Make incentive valuable enough to motivate (5-10% off works)
- Track incentive costs vs review value gained

### DON'T
- Offer incentive only for 5-star reviews (review manipulation - illegal on Amazon, unethical everywhere)
- Require positive language ("tell us what you loved")
- Incentivize reviews before product is received/used
- Make incentive contingent on review being published (some platforms prohibit this)

## Platform-specific rules

### Amazon
- **NO incentives allowed** - Amazon's policy prohibits incentivized reviews entirely
- Exception: Vine program (Amazon's own early reviewer program)
- Request a Review button = automated, no incentive

### Shopify/own site
- Incentives allowed if disclosed ("we offer discount for honest reviews")
- Best practice: incentive for review, not rating
- Apps like Judge.me have built-in incentive features

### Flipkart/Meesho
- Incentives vary by platform - check seller policies
- Generally more permissive than Amazon
- Still avoid rating-specific incentives

## Measuring incentive ROI

```
Cost per review = (Incentive cost x reviews requested) / reviews received
Value per review = (Conversion lift from reviews) x (Average order value)

Example:
- 100 review requests x 10% off next order = ₹50 avg incentive cost x 100 = ₹5,000
- 40 reviews received = ₹125 per review
- If reviews increase conversion by 20% on ₹999 AOV product = ₹200 extra revenue per sale
- 10 extra sales from reviews = ₹10,000 revenue
- ROI = (₹10,000 - ₹5,000) / ₹5,000 = 100% ROI
```
