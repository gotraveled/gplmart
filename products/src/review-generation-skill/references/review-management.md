# Review management - responding + handling negatives

## Responding to positive reviews

### Template
```
Thank you so much for this kind review, [Name]! 🙏

[Address specific point they mentioned - "glad the fit works perfectly for you"]

We love hearing this - it makes our day. Hope to see you again soon!

[Store name]
```

### Rules
- Respond within 24-48 hours (shows you care)
- Personalize (mention their name, specific product)
- Keep it short (2-3 sentences)
- Invite them back ("come back for [new product]")

## Responding to negative reviews

### The framework
```
1. Acknowledge: "We're sorry to hear about your experience"
2. Apologize: "This isn't the experience we want you to have"
3. Offer solution: "We'd love to make this right - please DM/email us at [contact]"
4. Take offline: move conversation to private channel
```

### Template
```
Hi [Name], we're really sorry to hear about your experience with [product]. This isn't the quality we aim for.

We've sent you a DM/email to resolve this - please check your messages. We'd love the chance to make it right.

Thank you for bringing this to our attention.

[Store name]
```

### Rules
- Never argue publicly (future customers are watching)
- Never be defensive ("you're wrong" = lose the customer AND future customers)
- Offer concrete solution (refund, replacement, discount)
- Take conversation offline ASAP
- Follow up after resolution ("hope we resolved this - we'd love another chance")

## Handling review patterns

### Multiple similar negative reviews
- Product issue? → check quality, fix product
- Expectation issue? → update product description/photos
- Shipping issue? → review logistics partner
- Fake reviews? → report to platform, document evidence

### Review bombing (coordinated attack)
- Document timestamps, patterns
- Report to platform with evidence
- Respond professionally to each (don't engage in argument)
- Focus on genuine customer reviews

## Review schema (SEO benefit)

Add review structured data to product pages:
```json
{
  "@type": "Product",
  "aggregateRating": {
    "@type": "AggregateRating",
    "ratingValue": "4.8",
    "reviewCount": "150"
  }
}
```

This enables rich snippets in Google = higher CTR = more traffic.
