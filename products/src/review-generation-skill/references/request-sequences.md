# Request sequences - full templates

## Email sequence (3 emails over 21 days)

### Email 1 - Initial request (day 7-10)

```
Subject: How's your [product] working out?
Preview: We'd love to hear your thoughts.

Hi [Name],

Hope you're enjoying your [product]! It's been a week since delivery - how's everything going?

Mind sharing a quick review? It helps other customers make better decisions + earns you [incentive if offered].

[Review link]

Takes 2 minutes, makes a big difference. Thanks!

[Store name]
```

### Email 2 - Follow-up (day 14)

```
Subject: Quick reminder - review for [product]
Preview: Even a sentence helps other buyers.

Hi [Name],

Just bumping this in case it got buried - would love your feedback on [product].

Even a short review helps other customers decide. [Review link]

No worries if you're busy - thanks anyway for being a customer!

[Store name]
```

### Email 3 - Final attempt (day 21)

```
Subject: Last ask - [product] review
Preview: If you have 30 seconds, we'd appreciate it.

Hi [Name],

Last reminder about the review for your [product]. If you have 30 seconds, it would mean a lot.

[Review link]

Either way, thanks for choosing [Store] - hope you love it!

[Store name]
```
