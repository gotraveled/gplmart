# Bundle types - detailed breakdown

## 1. Pure bundle (only sold together)

**What**: Products that only make sense as a set  
**Example**: Skincare routine (cleanser + toner + moisturizer + sunscreen)  
**Price**: 15-20% off buying separately  
**Best for**: Complete solutions, routines, kits  
**Platform**: Shopify Bundles app, manual bundle product

**Why it works**: Customer can't buy individually = forced bundle, higher perceived value ("complete routine"), simplifies decision (one purchase vs four)

## 2. Mixed bundle (buy X get Y discount)

**What**: Buy product A, get discount on complementary product B  
**Example**: "Buy laptop, get 15% off accessories" or "Buy 2 shirts get 20% off pants"  
**Price**: 10-15% off the add-on product  
**Best for**: Cross-selling categories, increasing items per order  
**Platform**: Shopify Bundle apps, cart upsell apps

**Why it works**: Increases AOV without heavy discounting on main product, introduces customers to other categories, feels like a deal not a bundle

## 3. Quantity break

**What**: Price decreases as quantity increases  
**Example**: Buy 1 = ₹499, Buy 2 = ₹899 (10% off), Buy 3 = ₹1,199 (20% off)  
**Price**: 10-20% off for bulk  
**Best for**: Consumables, gifts, B2B, wholesale  
**Platform**: Shopify quantity break apps, variant pricing

**Why it works**: Bulk buyers save, you move volume, consumables reorder (customer stocks up), increases AOV naturally

## 4. Gift bundle

**What**: Buy product, get free gift or gift-wrapping  
**Example**: "Buy serum, get free applicator" or "Gift set: product + wrapping + card"  
**Price**: Gift item cost only (not discount on main product)  
**Best for**: Gifting season, new customer acquisition, perceived value boost  
**Platform**: Shopify automatic discounts, gift with purchase apps

**Why it works**: Adds perceived value without discounting main product, perfect for gifting occasions, creates urgency ("while gift lasts")

## 5. Mystery/surprise bundle

**What**: Bundle of products, contents partially hidden  
**Example**: "Surprise bundle - 3 products for ₹999" (worth ₹1,500)  
**Price**: 30-40% off total value  
**Best for**: Moving slow stock, creating excitement, impulse buys  
**Platform**: Manual bundle product, mystery box apps

**Why it works**: Excitement of surprise, moves slow movers, creates urgency ("while stock lasts"), higher perceived value ("worth ₹1,500!")

## 6. Seasonal bundle

**What**: Themed bundles for festivals/seasons  
**Example**: Diwali gift set, summer care kit, back-to-school pack, wedding season package  
**Price**: 15-25% off individual items  
**Best for**: Seasonal sales, gift-giving, clearing seasonal stock  
**Platform**: Any - create as limited-time bundle products

**Why it works**: Timely relevance, gift-giving angle, clears seasonal stock before next season, creates urgency ("Diwali special")
