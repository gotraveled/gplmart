# Performance tracking - metrics + optimization

## Key metrics

| Metric | What it tells you | Good benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Bundle AOV vs single AOV | Is bundling increasing order value? | Bundle AOV 20-50% higher |
| Bundle conversion rate | Do bundles convert better or worse? | Within 10% of single product conversion |
| Bundle margin | Are bundles profitable? | Maintain minimum margin target |
| Bundle attach rate | How often is bundle chosen over single? | 15-30% of orders include bundle |
| Bundle revenue share | What % of revenue comes from bundles? | 20-40% for mature stores |
| Bundle product mix | Which bundles perform best? | Identify winners to replicate |

## Analysis template

```
BUNDLE PERFORMANCE - [Month]

Total bundle orders: [X]
Bundle revenue: ₹[X] ([Y]% of total revenue)
Bundle AOV: ₹[X] vs Single AOV: ₹[Y]

Top performing bundles:
1. [Bundle name] - [X] orders - ₹[X] revenue - [Z]% margin
2. [Bundle name] - [X] orders - ₹[X] revenue - [Z]% margin

Underperforming bundles:
1. [Bundle name] - [X] orders - low conversion - action: [kill/adjust]

Actions taken:
- [what changed]

Next month focus:
- [what to test/optimize]
```

## Optimization actions

### Kill underperforming bundles
- Low conversion (<2% of visitors) + low margin = kill
- Replace with new bundle concept
- Don't let dead bundles clutter catalog

### Double down on winners
- High conversion + good margin = promote harder
- Feature on homepage, email, ads
- Create variations (different product combos)

### Test bundle prices
- A/B test 10% vs 15% vs 20% off
- Measure conversion + margin (not just conversion)
- Winner = higher profit, not just higher sales

### Test bundle compositions
- Which products work together? (complements vs random)
- Does adding slow mover as bonus increase conversion?
- Does removing weak product improve bundle appeal?

### Seasonal refresh
- Update bundles for festivals/trends
- Clear seasonal bundles before next season
- Create new seasonal bundles proactively

## Common mistakes

- Bundling unrelated products (confuses customer)
- Discount too deep (kills margin)
- Too many bundle options (decision paralysis)
- Not promoting bundles (created but nobody knows)
- Not tracking performance (don't know what works)
