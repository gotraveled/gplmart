# Promotion tactics - on-site, email, ads

## On-site promotion

### Homepage
- Banner: "Bundle & Save 15% - This Week Only"
- Featured collection: "Bundles" or "Complete Kits"
- Hero image showing bundle with savings callout

### Product pages
- "Frequently bought together" section (apps or manual)
- "Complete the look" for fashion
- "You may also need" for complementary products
- Bundle savings callout: "Save ₹150 when bought together"

### Cart page
- "Complete your order with..." upsell section
- "Add [complementary product] for just ₹X more"
- Free shipping progress bar (encourage higher cart value)

### Collection pages
- Dedicated "Bundles" collection
- Filter by "Bundle & Save"
- Bundle badges on product images

## Email/WhatsApp promotion

### Broadcast
```
🔥 BUNDLE DEAL - Save 15% This Week! 🔥

[Bundle name]: [Product A] + [Product B] + [Product C]
Individual price: ₹1,000
Bundle price: ₹850 (save ₹150!)

[Link]

Valid till [date] - while stock lasts.
```

### Post-purchase
```
Thanks for your order! 🎉

Customers who bought [product] also love [complementary product].

Bundle them together and save 15%: [link]
```

### Win-back
```
We miss you! Here's a special bundle just for you:

[Bundle name] - ₹850 (was ₹1,000)

[Link]

Valid for [X] days only.
```

## Ads promotion

### Ad creative
- Show all bundle items + total savings
- "Buy X get Y 15% off" in ad copy
- Before/after: "₹1,000 → ₹850"
- UGC-style: customer unboxing the bundle

### Landing pages
- Dedicated bundle landing page for ad traffic
- Clear bundle contents + savings
- Single CTA (add bundle to cart)
- Social proof (reviews of bundle)

### Retargeting
- Show bundle to cart abandoners ("complete your order and save")
- Show bundle to past purchasers ("customers also bought")
- Show bundle to product viewers ("frequently bought together")

## Pricing psychology in promotion

- Anchor: show individual prices next to bundle price
- Savings framing: "Save ₹150" better than "15% off"
- Scarcity: "Limited bundle - only X sets" (if real)
- Urgency: real deadlines only ("ends Sunday" needs actual end)
- Social proof: "Bestseller bundle - 500+ sold" (if true)
