# Pricing strategy - discount depth + perceived value

## Discount depth guide

| Bundle type | Discount depth | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pure bundle | 15-20% off | Complete solution justifies higher discount |
| Mixed bundle | 10-15% off add-on | Cross-sell, not main product discount |
| Quantity break | 10% (2x), 20% (3x+) | Bulk incentive, volume play |
| Gift bundle | Gift cost only | Adds value without discounting main product |
| Mystery bundle | 30-40% off value | Excitement + moving slow stock |
| Seasonal bundle | 15-25% off | Timely urgency + gift angle |

## Perceived value tactics

### Anchoring
- Show "was ₹1,000, now ₹850" (was price = individual purchase total)
- List individual prices next to bundle price ("₹500 + ₹300 + ₹200 = ₹1,000 → Bundle: ₹850")
- "Save ₹150" framing better than "15% off" (concrete vs percentage)

### Value stacking
- List everything included: "Get: Product A + Product B + Product C + Free shipping"
- Show individual values: "Total value ₹1,200, you pay ₹999"
- Add bonuses: "Plus free [gift] on orders over ₹999"

### Scarcity/urgency
- "Limited bundle - only 50 sets available" (if real)
- "This bundle ends Sunday" (real deadline only)
- "While stock lasts" (only if stock is genuinely limited)

### Social proof
- "Bestseller bundle - 500+ sold"
- "Customers love this combo" (if true)
- "Perfect gift - 4.8★ rated"

## Bundle price points

Create bundles at psychological price points:
- ₹499 bundle (impulse buy, entry-level)
- ₹999 bundle (most popular - sweet spot for Indian ecommerce)
- ₹1,499 bundle (premium, higher margin)
- ₹1,999+ bundle (gift/premium positioning)

## What NOT to do

- Don't discount bestsellers unnecessarily (they sell at full price)
- Don't bundle unrelated products (confuses customer, hurts conversion)
- Don't make bundles too complex (3-4 items max for clarity)
- Don't hide bundle savings (be transparent about the deal)
- Don't run evergreen bundle discounts (>30 days = becomes expected price)
