## The 14-day store launch plan

### Days 1-3: Foundation
1. **Set up policies** - fill every [BRACKET] in the Document Pack (privacy, refund, shipping, T&C). Paste into Shopify admin: Settings > Policies.
2. **Write your FAQ** - customize faq-template.md, publish as a page, link in footer.
3. **Set up email templates** - order confirmation, shipping, review request. Install in your email app (Klaviyo, Shopify Email, etc).

### Days 4-7: Pricing & math
4. **Build your Product Costs sheet** - every product's real cost: material, shipping, fees, ad spend.
5. **Run Pricing Calculator** on your hero product - find break-even and target price.
6. **Set up P&L Dashboard** - you'll log monthly numbers starting week 3.

### Days 8-11: Marketing engine
7. **Write 10 ad hooks** from the Swipe File - adapt [PRODUCT]/[AUDIENCE] placeholders.
8. **Load the Social Media Calendar** - batch-create week 1's posts from captions.csv.
9. **Set up your LinkedIn/Instagram** - use the Resume Kit's profile tips if freelancing.

### Days 12-14: Soft launch
10. **Soft launch to friends/family** - get 5-10 real orders.
11. **Log everything** in the Profit Tracker - real conversion data beats guesses.
12. **Review your policies page** - make sure contact info and policies are linked everywhere.

> **KEY INSIGHT:** The stores that survive past 90 days aren't the ones with the best products - they're the ones with the best systems. This bundle IS the system.
