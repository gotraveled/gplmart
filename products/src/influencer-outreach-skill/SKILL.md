# Influencer outreach - find creators who actually sell

Influencer marketing fails when you chase followers instead of fit. This skill finds creators
whose audience actually buys your product category, writes outreach that gets responses,
structures campaigns that track ROI, and manages relationships that become repeat partners.

Works for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and emerging platforms. India-focused but globally applicable.

---

## Intake - one batch

1. Product/store + what you're selling + price point
2. Goal: product launch / ongoing sales / brand awareness / UGC content / affiliate program
3. Budget: per-post rate you're willing to pay OR gifting-only
4. Target geography: India / global / specific cities
5. Product category + target audience (age, gender, interests, income)
6. Campaign timeline: one-time / ongoing / seasonal
7. Any constraints: exclusivity requirements, content approval needs, usage rights

## The creator evaluation framework

### Red flags (skip these)

- Follower count high but engagement <1% (bought followers)
- Comments are generic ("nice", "love it", "🔥") = bot engagement
- Content doesn't match your category (fashion creator promoting kitchen gadgets)
- Recent posts show competitor products (conflict of interest)
- No contact info / only "email for collab" with no business email

### Green flags (prioritize these)

- Engagement rate >3% (Instagram) / >5% (TikTok) / >2% (YouTube)
- Comments show real conversations (questions, recommendations, personal stories)
- Content quality consistent - they actually try products, not just hold them
- Audience matches your buyer (check comment demographics if visible)
- Has done paid partnerships before (knows how to disclose #ad properly)
- Response time reasonable (replies to comments within hours)

### The vetting checklist per creator

```
CREATOR: [handle]
PLATFORM: [IG/TikTok/YT]
FOLLOWERS: [count]
ENGAGEMENT RATE: [%]
AUDIENCE FIT: [does their audience match your buyer?]
CONTENT QUALITY: [would you want YOUR product shown this way?]
PAST COLLABS: [visible #ad posts? quality of those?]
CONTACT: [email/DM manager]
RATE: [if visible or after inquiry]
VERDICT: [yes/no/maybe]
```

## Discovery methods (free + paid)

### Free methods

1. **Instagram/TikTok search**: search product category + "review", "unboxing", "honest review"
2. **Competitor analysis**: who do competitors work with? (check their tagged posts, #ad content)
3. **Hashtag research**: #[product category] + #[city] + "creator" / "influencer"
4. **Google**: "[product category] influencer India" / "top [niche] creators"
5. **Your customers**: who posts about your products already? (UGC creators are cheapest)

### Paid tools (if budget allows)

- **Collabstr / Insense / Upfluence**: creator marketplaces with rates + analytics
- **HypeAuditor**: fake follower detection + audience analysis
- **Modash / Heepsy**: Instagram/TikTok creator search with filters

## Outreach that gets responses

### The first message (DM or email)

```
Hi [Name]! I love your content - especially your recent [specific post/video about X].

I'm [Your Name] from [Store] - we make [product] for [audience].

We'd love to send you [product] to try (no obligation to post). If you love it, we'd be happy to discuss a paid collaboration.

Would you be interested? Happy to share more details.

[Your name + store link]
```

Rules:
- Reference SPECIFIC content (shows you actually watched/read)
- Lead with gifting before asking for payment (lower barrier)
- Keep it under 100 words
- No attachments, no links in first message (spam filters)
- Follow up ONCE after 5-7 days, then let it go

### The follow-up (5-7 days later)

```
Hey [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried!
Still happy to send [product] for you to try - no strings attached.
Let me know if you're interested! 🙏
```

## Negotiation & pricing (India benchmarks)

| Creator size | Instagram post | Instagram reel | TikTok video | YouTube integration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nano (1K-10K) | ₹500-2,000 | ₹1,000-3,000 | ₹500-2,000 | ₹2,000-5,000 |
| Micro (10K-50K) | ₹2,000-8,000 | ₹3,000-10,000 | ₹2,000-8,000 | ₹5,000-15,000 |
| Mid (50K-200K) | ₹8,000-25,000 | ₹10,000-30,000 | ₹8,000-25,000 | ₹15,000-50,000 |
| Macro (200K+) | ₹25,000+ | ₹30,000+ | ₹25,000+ | ₹50,000+ |

Negotiation tips:
- Start with gifting - creators who love the product often post organically
- For paid: offer product + fee, or fee-only for established creators
- Bundle deals: 3 posts for price of 2
- Affiliate option: commission-only for nano/micro creators (they take the risk)
- Usage rights cost extra - clarify if you want to reuse their content in ads

## Campaign briefs (what to give creators)

```
CAMPAIGN BRIEF
Product: [name + link]
Key selling points: [3 bullets]
Target audience: [who should see this]
Content requirements:
- Must show product in use (not just holding)
- Mention [key benefit] naturally
- Include [CTA - link in bio, discount code, etc.]
- Disclose as #ad / paid partnership
- Deliverables: [1 reel + 2 stories + 1 feed post]
Timeline: [post by date]
Compensation: [fee + product / affiliate commission]
```

## ROI tracking

### Unique discount codes

Give each creator a unique code: CREATOR10, PRIYA15, etc. Track which codes convert.

### UTM links

`?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=influencer&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=priya_reel`

### What to measure

- **Reach**: impressions/views (awareness)
- **Engagement**: likes, comments, shares, saves (content quality)
- **Clicks**: link clicks (interest)
- **Conversions**: sales attributed to code/UTM (ROI)
- **Content reuse**: can you use their video in your ads? (extra value)

### ROI formula

```
ROI = (Revenue from campaign - Total cost) / Total cost x 100
```

Total cost = creator fees + product costs + your time managing.
