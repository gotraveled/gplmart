# Outreach templates - every stage

## Stage 1: First contact (DM/email)

### Short version (DM)

```
Hi [Name]! Love your [specific content type] - especially your recent [post/video title].

I'm [Your Name] from [Store] - we make [product category]. Would love to send you [product] to try (no obligation). If you love it, we can discuss paid collab.

Interested?
```

### Detailed version (email)

```
Subject: Love your content - [product] collab?

Hi [Name],

I've been following your content for a while - your recent [specific post/video about X] really resonated with me. The way you [specific thing they did well] is exactly the kind of authentic content we value.

I'm [Your Name], founder of [Store]. We make [product] for [target audience].

I'd love to send you [product] to try out - completely no strings attached. If you enjoy it, we'd be thrilled to discuss a paid collaboration where you could share it with your audience.

Here's our store if you're curious: [link]

Would you be open to trying it?

Best,
[Your name]
[Store name]
```

## Stage 2: Follow-up (5-7 days, one only)

```
Hey [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried!
Still happy to send [product] for you to try - no strings attached.
Let me know if you're interested! 🙏
```

## Stage 3: After they say yes

```
Amazing! 🎉 So glad you're interested.

I'll send over [product details] - could you share your:
- Full name
- Shipping address (with pincode)
- Phone number

Once it arrives, take your time trying it. We'd love to see what you think - no posting required, just honest feedback.

Looking forward to it!
```

## Stage 4: After they receive product

```
Hope you're enjoying the [product]! No rush at all.

When you have a moment, we'd love to know:
- What you think of it
- If you'd be open to a paid collaboration (if so, what your rates are)

No pressure either way - just want honest feedback.
```

## Stage 5: Negotiation

```
Thanks for the honest feedback! We're really glad you liked it.

For a paid collaboration, our budget for [deliverables] is [amount]. We're flexible - what would work for you?

We can also discuss:
- Affiliate commission instead of flat fee
- Product + fee combo
- Long-term partnership rates

What do you think?
```

## Stage 6: Campaign brief delivery

```
Perfect! Here's everything you need:

PRODUCT: [name] - [link]
KEY POINTS: [3 selling points]
TARGET AUDIENCE: [who should see this]

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS:
- Show product in use (not just holding)
- Mention [key benefit] naturally
- CTA: [what you want viewers to do]
- Disclose #ad / paid partnership

DELIVERABLES: [list]
TIMELINE: Post by [date]
COMPENSATION: [fee] + [product already sent]

Let me know if you have questions!
```
