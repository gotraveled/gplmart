# Campaign readiness checklist

## Before outreach

- [ ] Product ready (in stock, good photos, clear description)
- [ ] Offer decided (discount code, free shipping, gift with purchase)
- [ ] Budget allocated (creator fees + product + shipping)
- [ ] Target creator profile defined (niche, size, geography, engagement)
- [ ] Outreach templates customized for your brand voice
- [ ] Tracking set up (discount codes, UTM links, or affiliate tool)

## During outreach

- [ ] Creators vetted (engagement, audience fit, content quality)
- [ ] First message personalized (references their specific content)
- [ ] Gifting offered before paid ask
- [ ] Follow-up sent once after 5-7 days
- [ ] Responses tracked (spreadsheet or CRM)

## After they say yes

- [ ] Shipping details collected (address, phone)
- [ ] Product shipped with tracking
- [ ] Thank-you message sent
- [ ] Check-in after 3-5 days (how's the product?)
- [ ] Collaboration terms discussed (if they're interested)

## Campaign execution

- [ ] Campaign brief sent (deliverables, timeline, requirements)
- [ ] Content reviewed before posting (if contract allows)
- [ ] #ad disclosure verified
- [ ] Links/codes tested (working, tracking correctly)
- [ ] Posting deadline confirmed

## Post-campaign

- [ ] Content performance tracked (reach, engagement, clicks, sales)
- [ ] ROI calculated
- [ ] Creator relationship maintained (thank you, future opportunities)
- [ ] Learnings documented (what worked, what didn't)
- [ ] UGC content archived for ad reuse (if rights allow)
