## Negotiation scripts

### When they quote high

```
"Thanks for sharing your rates! Our budget for this campaign is [your budget]. 
We'd love to work with you - would [lower amount] for [deliverables] work for you?

Alternatively, we could do [product + smaller fee] or [affiliate commission].
What would you be comfortable with?"
```

### When they won't negotiate

```
"Totally understand! We'd love to keep you in mind for future campaigns.
If you're open to it, we could start with a gifted collaboration - you try the product,
and if you love it, share honestly with your audience. No posting required.

Would that interest you?"
```

### Bundle negotiation

```
"For [3 posts / 1 reel + 2 stories], our budget is [amount].
That's [X]% below your usual rate - would you consider it as a package deal?
We can also discuss long-term rates if this works well."
```

## Payment terms

- **Upfront**: 50% advance, 50% on delivery - standard
- **Net 7/15**: payment after post goes live - some creators accept for trusted brands
- **Product only**: for nano/micro creators who love your product
- **Affiliate**: commission-only (10-20% of sales) - risk-free for you, good for nano creators
- **Never pay full upfront without contract** - especially for first-time collaborations

## Contract essentials (even for small deals)

- Deliverables: exact posts, stories, reels, deadlines
- Usage rights: can you reuse their content in ads? (usually +20-50% fee)
- Exclusivity: can't promote competitors for X days (usually +10-20% fee)
- Disclosure: must use #ad / paid partnership tag
- Revision policy: 1-2 rounds of edits max
- Payment terms: amount, method, timeline
- Cancellation: what happens if either party backs out

Get it in writing (WhatsApp/email) even for small deals - protects both sides.
