# Creator vetting scorecard

## Scoring system (rate each 1-5)

| Criteria | 1 (bad) | 3 (ok) | 5 (great) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engagement rate | <1% | 2-3% | >5% |
| Content quality | Random/low effort | Consistent decent | Professional/polished |
| Audience fit | Wrong demographic | Some overlap | Exact match |
| Authenticity | Bot comments | Mixed | Real conversations |
| Niche relevance | Different category | Adjacent | Direct match |
| Past collabs | None/fake | Some, mediocre | Quality #ad content |

**Total 30 = perfect. 18+ = worth pursuing. Below 15 = skip.**

## Platform-specific checks

### Instagram
- Check last 20 posts: how many are #ad? (5-10 = professional creator)
- Read comments: real questions? emoji-only? "great post!" generic?
- Story views vs follower count: >10% = engaged audience
- Reels performance: do reels get more views than feed? (good for product promotion)

### TikTok
- Watch 3-5 videos: is the creator on-camera or faceless?
- Check comment quality: duets/stitches = engaged community
- Follower-to-like ratio: likes should be 3-10% of followers
- Trending sounds usage = algorithm-friendly content

### YouTube
- Check video views vs subscriber count: >30% = engaged
- Read top comments: real questions or spam?
- Video production quality: good editing = professional
- Integration style: natural mention vs hard sell?

## Audience verification (when possible)

- Ask for screenshot of audience insights (Instagram professional account shows this)
- Check "top locations" - matches your target geography?
- Check "top age/gender" - matches your buyer?
- Check "accounts reached" - are they real accounts or bots?

## The 10-minute vetting process

1. Open profile → check bio, link, contact
2. Scroll last 20 posts → engagement quality, content style
3. Read 10 comments → real or bot?
4. Check 1-2 #ad posts → how do they handle paid content?
5. Quick audience check → demographics match?
6. Score it → 18+ = reach out, <15 = move on
