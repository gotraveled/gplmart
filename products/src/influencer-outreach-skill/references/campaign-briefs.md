# Campaign briefs - templates per campaign type

## Product launch campaign

```
CAMPAIGN: [Product Name] Launch
GOAL: Awareness + first sales
TIMELINE: [2-week campaign window]

PRODUCT: [name] - [link]
KEY SELLING POINTS:
- [point 1]
- [point 2]  
- [point 3]

TARGET AUDIENCE: [age, gender, interests, location]

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS:
- Show product unboxing/first impression
- Demonstrate [key feature] in use
- Mention [specific benefit] naturally
- Include CTA: [link in bio / discount code / "comment below"]
- Must disclose #ad / paid partnership

DELIVERABLES PER CREATOR:
- 1 Instagram Reel OR TikTok video (15-30s)
- 2 Instagram Stories (product mention + link)
- Optional: 1 feed post

COMPENSATION: [fee] + product
TIMELINE: Posts live by [date]
USAGE RIGHTS: [can you reuse content in ads? +fee if yes]
```

## Ongoing affiliate program

```
ONGOING PARTNERSHIP

PRODUCT: [name/category]
COMMISSION: [10-20]% per sale via unique code/link
COOKIE WINDOW: [30 days]

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Creator shares unique link/code
2. Customer buys → creator gets commission
3. Monthly payout via [UPI/bank transfer]

REQUIREMENTS:
- Disclose affiliate relationship (#ad or "affiliate link")
- Post at least [X] times per month
- No misleading claims about product

PERKS:
- Early access to new products
- Exclusive discount codes for their audience
- Bonus commission on hitting [X] sales/month
```

## UGC content campaign (for ads)

```
UGC CONTENT CAMPAIGN

GOAL: Raw, authentic content for paid ads
PRODUCT: [name] - send product, no posting required

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS:
- 3-5 raw videos (not polished, phone-shot is fine)
- Show product in real use
- No scripting needed - just genuine reaction
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Can be vertical (9:16) for Reels/TikTok ads

COMPENSATION: [flat fee per video] + product
USAGE RIGHTS: Full rights to use in paid ads (this is why fee is higher)
DELIVERABLES: Raw footage files via Drive/WeTransfer
```

## Seasonal campaign (Diwali, Eid, etc.)

```
SEASONAL CAMPAIGN: [Festival Name]
THEME: [festive gifting / celebration / tradition]
PRODUCT FOCUS: [giftable products]

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS:
- Show product as gift or in festive context
- Mention [festival-specific angle]
- Include offer: [discount code / free shipping]
- Warm, celebratory tone (not hard sell)

TIMELINE: Posts live [X days before festival]
COMPENSATION: [premium rate - seasonal demand is higher]
```
