# Social media calendar - consistent content without burnout

Social media fails when it's sporadic. This skill builds the SYSTEM: content pillars, posting
schedule, caption templates and campaign themes that keep your store visible without daily
scrambling for ideas.

Output = a 30-day calendar with post ideas, captions, hashtags and visual direction per platform.

---

## Intake - one batch

1. Store/product + what you sell + price range
2. Platforms active: Instagram / Facebook / TikTok / Pinterest / WhatsApp Status / YouTube Shorts
3. Current state: posting daily / weekly / sporadically / not at all
4. Content assets: product photos / videos / UGC / can create new / need ideas
5. Team capacity: solo founder / VA / team (affects realistic posting frequency)
6. Goals: sales / brand awareness / community / all three
7. Any campaigns coming: sales, festivals, product launches

## Platform strategy (India ecommerce)

| Platform | Best for | Frequency | Content type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Brand building, product showcase, community | 1 feed + 3-5 stories/day | Reels, carousels, product shots |
| Facebook | Reach, community, older demographics | 1 post/day | Product posts, offers, videos |
| TikTok | Viral reach, younger audience | 1-3 videos/day | Short-form video, trends |
| Pinterest | Evergreen traffic, SEO | 5-10 pins/day | Product pins, lifestyle boards |
| WhatsApp Status | Direct customer reach | 2-3/day | Offers, restocks, behind-scenes |
| YouTube Shorts | Search + discovery | 1-3/day | Product demos, tutorials |

## Content pillars (the 5 buckets)

Every post fits ONE pillar - this prevents random posting:

### 1. Product showcase (30%)
- Product photos, videos, features, benefits
- "Product of the week" spotlights
- New arrivals, bestsellers, bundles

### 2. Educational/value (25%)
- How-to guides, tips, tricks
- "Did you know?" facts about your category
- Styling/usage/how-to-use content
- Comparison posts (your product vs alternatives)

### 3. Social proof (20%)
- Customer reviews, testimonials, UGC
- Unboxing videos, customer photos
- "Customer of the week" features
- Before/after (where applicable)

### 4. Offers/promotions (15%)
- Sales, discounts, bundles, free shipping
- Flash sales, clearance, seasonal offers
- Limited-time deals, early access for followers

### 5. Behind-the-scenes/brand (10%)
- Team, process, packaging, shipping
- Founder story, mission, values
- "Day in the life" content
- Packing orders, warehouse tours

## The 30-day calendar structure

### Week 1: Foundation
- Monday: Product showcase (hero product)
- Tuesday: Educational (tip related to product)
- Wednesday: Social proof (customer review)
- Thursday: Product showcase (different angle)
- Friday: Offer (weekend sale or bundle)
- Saturday: Behind-scenes (packing orders)
- Sunday: Educational or rest day

### Week 2-4: Repeat with variations
- Same pillars, different products/content
- Theme each week: "Kitchen week", "Best sellers week", "Customer love week"

### Special days to plan for
- Republic Day (Jan 26), Holi, Diwali, Eid, Raksha Bandhan, etc.
- Your store anniversary, product launch dates
- Trending topics/memes relevant to your niche

## Hashtag strategy

### Per platform

- **Instagram**: 15-20 hashtags (mix of size: big/medium/niche)
- **Facebook**: 3-5 hashtags max
- **TikTok**: 3-5 hashtags + trending sounds
- **Pinterest**: keywords in description, not hashtags

### Hashtag categories

- **Broad**: #shopping #fashion #kitchen (high volume, low targeting)
- **Niche**: #lunchbox #tiffinlove #homedecor (medium volume, good targeting)
- **Branded**: #[yourbrand] #yourbrandindia (build community)
- **Trending**: check daily, only if relevant to your niche

## Batching & scheduling

### Weekly batch (2-3 hours)

1. Pick 7 posts for the week (one per pillar per day)
2. Write captions for all 7
3. Create/edit visuals (photos, graphics, videos)
4. Schedule via Meta Business Suite / Later / Buffer
5. Engage with comments daily (15 min/day)

### Monthly planning (1 hour)

1. Review last month's performance (what worked?)
2. Plan campaign themes for the month
3. Identify key dates/events to post around
4. Create content backlog (aim for 2 weeks ahead)
