# Hashtag strategy - research + implementation

## Hashtag categories

### Broad hashtags (high volume, low targeting)
- #shopping #fashion #kitchen #home #gifts #sale
- Volume: millions of posts
- Use: 2-3 per post for reach

### Niche hashtags (medium volume, good targeting)
- #lunchbox #tiffinlove #homedecor #indianfashion #giftingideas
- Volume: thousands to hundreds of thousands
- Use: 5-8 per post for targeted reach

### Branded hashtags (your community)
- #[yourbrand] #yourbrighthashtag #yourbrandindia
- Volume: yours to build
- Use: 1-2 per post to build community

### Trending hashtags (check daily)
- Only use if relevant to your niche
- Check Instagram "Explore" page and TikTok trending
- Use within 24-48 hours of trending

## Research methods (free)

### Instagram
1. Search your product category → check "Tags" tab for related hashtags
2. Look at top posts in your niche → what hashtags do they use?
3. Check hashtag volume (search hashtag → see post count)
4. Use Instagram's suggested hashtags when writing captions

### TikTok
1. Check trending sounds/hashes on For You page
2. Search your niche → see what hashtags top videos use
3. Use 3-5 hashtags max (more = spammy)

### Pinterest
- Pinterest is a search engine - use keywords in description, not hashtags
- Create boards with keyword-rich titles

## Hashtag rules

- Instagram: 15-20 hashtags per post (in caption or first comment)
- Facebook: 3-5 hashtags max
- TikTok: 3-5 hashtags + trending sounds
- Pinterest: keywords in description, not hashtags
- Never use banned/spammy hashtags (#like4like, #followforfollow)

## Rotation strategy

- Don't use same 20 hashtags every post (looks spammy)
- Create 3-4 hashtag sets, rotate them
- Update hashtag sets monthly based on performance
- Track which hashtags drive most profile visits/saves
