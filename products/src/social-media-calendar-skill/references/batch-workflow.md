# Batch workflow - weekly + monthly production

## Weekly batch (2-3 hours, once per week)

### Step 1: Plan (30 min)
- Review last week's performance (which posts got engagement?)
- Pick 7 posts for the week (one per pillar)
- Check calendar for any special dates/events
- Assign themes to each day

### Step 2: Create content (60-90 min)
- Take/edit product photos OR select from existing library
- Create simple graphics (Canva templates work well)
- Film/edit videos (Reels, TikToks, Shorts)
- Write all 7 captions
- Prepare hashtags per platform

### Step 3: Schedule (30 min)
- Use Meta Business Suite (free, Instagram + Facebook)
- Or Later/Buffer/Hootsuite (paid, multi-platform)
- Schedule all posts for the week
- Set reminders for engagement time

### Step 4: Daily engagement (15 min/day)
- Respond to comments on posts
- Reply to DMs
- Engage with relevant accounts (comment on others' posts)
- Post stories throughout the day

## Monthly planning (1 hour, once per month)

### Step 1: Review (20 min)
- What performed best last month? (reach, engagement, sales)
- What flopped? (why?)
- Which platform drove most traffic/sales?
- Any patterns (certain content types, times, topics)?

### Step 2: Plan themes (20 min)
- Pick 4 weekly themes for the month
- Identify key dates: festivals, sales, product launches
- Plan campaign moments (Diwali sale, new collection drop)
- Set monthly goals (followers, engagement, sales)

### Step 3: Create content backlog (20 min)
- Batch-create 2 weeks of content ahead
- Build content library (photos, videos, captions)
- Prepare campaign assets (sale graphics, offer codes)

## Content batching tips

- **Photo day**: shoot all product photos in one session (2-3 hours)
- **Video day**: film all Reels/TikToks in one session
- **Caption day**: write all captions in one sitting
- **Schedule day**: load everything into scheduler

## Tools

- **Free**: Meta Business Suite, Canva, Google Sheets (calendar)
- **Paid**: Later, Buffer, Hootsuite, Planoly (Instagram-focused)
- **Analytics**: Instagram Insights, Facebook Analytics, TikTok Analytics
