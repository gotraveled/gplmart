# Platform strategy - per-platform best practices

## Instagram

### Feed posts
- Best times: 11am-1pm, 7pm-9pm (IST)
- Formats: carousel (5-10 slides) for education, single image for products, Reels for reach
- Captions: hook in first line, keywords for SEO, 15-20 hashtags
- Visual: consistent color palette, good lighting, clean backgrounds

### Stories (3-5/day)
- Polls/questions for engagement
- Product tags with links
- Countdown stickers for sales
- Behind-scenes, quick updates
- "Add yours" sticker chains

### Reels
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Trending audio (check weekly)
- Text overlays for silent viewing
- CTA at end (follow, comment, link)
- Post 3-5x/week minimum for growth

## Facebook

### Posts
- Best times: 9am-11am, 1pm-3pm, 7pm-9pm
- Formats: image + caption, video, link posts
- Longer captions OK (up to 2,000 chars)
- 3-5 hashtags max
- Community-focused content works well

### Groups
- Join relevant groups (local buy/sell, niche communities)
- Share value first, promote rarely (80/20 rule)
- Respond to comments quickly

## TikTok

### Videos
- Hook in first 1-3 seconds (critical)
- Trending sounds increase reach
- 15-60 seconds optimal for product content
- Text overlays for silent viewing
- Post 1-3x/day for growth
- Engage with comments in first hour

### Strategy
- Repurpose Instagram Reels content
- Follow trends but adapt to your niche
- Behind-scenes content performs well
- Educational/how-to content gets saved

## Pinterest

### Pins
- Best times: 8pm-11pm, weekends
- Vertical images (2:3 ratio) perform best
- Keyword-rich descriptions (Pinterest is a search engine)
- Create boards by category/use case
- 5-10 pins/day for consistent growth
- Link directly to product pages

## WhatsApp Status

### Posts (2-3/day)
- Offers, restocks, new arrivals
- Behind-scenes, packing orders
- Customer reviews/testimonials
- Polls/questions for engagement
- Keep under 30 seconds per status
- Post during peak hours (12pm-2pm, 7pm-10pm)
