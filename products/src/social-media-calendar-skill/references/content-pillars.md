# Content pillars - detailed breakdown

## Pillar 1: Product Showcase (30%)

### Post types
- Hero product shot (clean, professional)
- Product in use (lifestyle, hands, context)
- Feature highlight (close-up of key feature)
- Comparison (your product vs generic alternative)
- Size/scale reference ("fits in hand", "fills the shelf")
- Bundle/collection showcase

### Caption angle
- Benefit-led: "Keeps food hot for 5 hours"
- Problem-solution: "Tired of cold lunch? This fixes it"
- Social proof: "10,000+ customers can't be wrong"

## Pillar 2: Educational/Value (25%)

### Post types
- How-to guides ("How to pack the perfect tiffin")
- Tips & tricks ("3 ways to extend the life of your [product]")
- Did-you-know facts ("Your lunch box could be making you sick")
- Comparison posts ("Steel vs plastic lunch boxes - which is better?")
- Myth-busting ("You don't need to wash your [product] daily")

### Caption angle
- Teach something useful
- Establish authority in your niche
- Save-worthy content ("Save this for later!")
- Shareable ("Tag someone who needs this tip")

## Pillar 3: Social Proof (20%)

### Post types
- Customer reviews (screenshot or quote graphic)
- UGC reposts (customer photos/videos with permission)
- Unboxing videos
- "Customer of the week" features
- Before/after (where applicable and honest)
- Testimonial carousels

### Caption angle
- Real quotes, real names
- "This made our day" energy
- Community feeling ("You're part of [X] families")
- Encourage more UGC ("Share your [product] setup!")

## Pillar 4: Offers/Promotions (15%)

### Post types
- Sale announcements (sitewide, category, product)
- Flash sales (24-48 hour urgency)
- Bundle deals (buy 2 get 1, combo offers)
- Free shipping thresholds
- Discount codes for followers
- Clearance/last-chance sales

### Caption angle
- Clear offer details (what, how much, when ends)
- Real urgency only ("ends Sunday" needs actual end date)
- Social proof ("selling fast", "almost gone")
- Easy CTA ("Link in bio", "Comment 'DEAL' for details")

## Pillar 5: Behind-the-Scenes/Brand (10%)

### Post types
- Packing orders (satisfying ASMR-style)
- Team/founder stories
- Warehouse/office tours
- Product creation process
- Shipping/delivery updates
- "Day in the life" content
- Packaging unboxing

### Caption angle
- Humanize the brand
- Build trust ("every order packed with care")
- Behind-the-curtain curiosity
- Gratitude ("thank you for supporting us")
