# The 30-Day Faith Journal: A Guided Devotional for Busy Hearts

A paid 30-day guided devotional journal for Christians — 30 daily entries, each with a scripture verse, a short reflection and three writing prompts (gratitude, prayer, lesson). Weekly themes: gratitude, prayer, scripture memory, rest & trust — plus month-end review pages. Denominationally neutral, usable by Protestants and Catholics.

## Contents
- book.md — full journal source (converts to book.pdf via scripts/md-to-pdf.mjs)
- verses.csv — 30 scripture references and texts (day, verse_reference, verse_text)
- prompts.csv — all 90 writing prompts (day, prompt_1, prompt_2, prompt_3)

## Usage
- **Print:** Print double-sided (flip on short edge) on A4 or Letter; bind, staple, or hole-punch — or keep it fully digital.
- **Daily:** 15 minutes — verse + reflection in the morning, prompts in the evening. Skip days freely; don't catch up.
- **Weeks:** 1 gratitude, 2 prayer, 3 scripture memory, 4 rest & trust, then a month-end review.
- Scripture quotations are KJV (public domain). Written for GPL-3.0-or-later.
