# Social Media Content Kit Vol. 1: Ecommerce Edition

**GPLMart — ready-to-post swipe files for Shopify and DTC brands**

You don't need more ideas. You need posts. This kit is a copy-paste engine: captions, hooks, hashtag banks, a 30-day calendar, and the review habits that turn posting into sales.

> **KEY INSIGHT:** Social commerce doesn't reward the most creative brand. It rewards the most consistent one. A mediocre caption posted every day beats a brilliant caption posted once a month.

---

## How to Use This Kit

Everything here is built around `[PRODUCT]` and `[BRAND]` placeholders. Swap in your own product name and brand voice and post. Save this guide as your reference — the CSVs are your working files.

### Setup in 30 minutes

1. Open `captions.csv`. Filter to the category you need.
2. Copy a caption. Replace `[PRODUCT]` and `[BRAND]`. Delete lines that don't fit.
3. Open `hooks.csv`. Pick a hook that matches your product's strongest angle.
4. Open `hashtags.csv`. Pick the set closest to your niche; swap 2–3 tags for your own branded tag.
5. Check `calendar.csv` for where this post sits in the 30-day arc.

> **PRO TIP:** Batch-create all captions for the week on Sunday. Spend 45 minutes editing instead of 20 minutes per day staring at a blank caption box.

### The 30-day rollout plan

Run this arc four times a year, or once monthly at lower intensity.

**Week 1 — Educate (build the "why")**

| Days | Theme | Job |
|------|-------|-----|
| 1–3 | Problem posts | Name the pain your customer already feels |
| 4–7 | Education posts | Teach something useful about the product category |

Week 1 earns follows. Nobody buys from a stranger; they buy from someone who taught them something.

**Week 2 — Social proof (build the "trust")**

| Days | Theme | Job |
|------|-------|-----|
| 8–10 | Reviews & UGC | Let customers sell for you |
| 11–14 | Behind the scenes | Show the humans and the process |

Week 2 earns trust. Screenshots of reviews, unboxing clips, and warehouse footage outperform polished ads in saves and shares.

**Week 3 — Offer (build the "buy")**

| Days | Theme | Job |
|------|-------|-----|
| 15–17 | Offer posts | Bundle, discount, gift-with-purchase |
| 18–21 | Urgency & objection handling | Answer "is it worth it?" publicly |

Week 3 converts. This is where the calendar's promo posts live — don't skip education weeks and only post offers, or the algorithm and your audience will both ignore you.

**Week 4 — Community (build the "habit")**

| Days | Theme | Job |
|------|-------|-----|
| 22–24 | Engagement | Questions, polls, "this or that" |
| 25–28 | Customer stories | Feature real buyers |
| 29–30 | Recap & next-cycle tease | Look back, tease what's coming |

> **KEY INSIGHT:** The funnel isn't linear — it's a loop. Week 4 community posts feed back into Week 1's audience pool. A buyer who comments in Week 4 is a warmer lead for Week 3's offer next cycle.

## Chapter 1: Instagram Caption Formulas

Instagram captions have five jobs. Pick the formula that matches the job.

### The five formulas

| Formula | Structure | Use when | Best metric |
|---------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| Save | Hook → value → "save this" | Educational content | Saves |
| Educational | Claim → explanation → proof → takeaway | Building authority | Shares, follows |
| Story | Scene → feeling → product → soft CTA | Lifestyle imagery | Comments, DMs |
| Promo | Pain → offer → urgency → CTA | Sales posts | Link clicks, orders |
| Question | Observation → question → reply prompt | Engagement | Comments |

> **KEY INSIGHT:** Instagram's algorithm weighs sends (DM shares) and saves more heavily than likes. Write captions that give people a reason to save — checklists, comparisons, "steal this" angles.

### Save captions (educational, bookmark-worthy)

```
POV: you finally found the [PRODUCT] that actually [OUTCOME].

Here's why it works:
1. [REASON 1]
2. [REASON 2]
3. [REASON 3]

Save this before you forget. Tag the friend who needs it. 👇
```

```
The [PRODUCT] buying guide nobody gave you:

→ [DIMENSION 1] matters because...
→ [DIMENSION 2] matters because...
→ Here's what to avoid at all costs.

Save + share with someone shopping right now.
```

```
3 signs your [CATEGORY] is the problem:

☐ [SIGN 1]
☐ [SIGN 2]
☐ [SIGN 3]

If you checked 2+, [PRODUCT] was built for you.

Save this checklist — you'll want it later.
```

### Educational captions (authority builders)

```
[MYTH] about [CATEGORY] is costing you money.

The truth? [FACT].

Most brands charge more for [THING] that doesn't actually [BENEFIT]. [BRAND] does it differently: [DIFFERENCE].

Drop a 🔥 if this surprised you.
```

```
We analyzed [NUMBER] reviews of [CATEGORY]. Here's what customers actually complained about:

1. [COMPLAINT 1]
2. [COMPLAINT 2]
3. [COMPLAINT 3]

Every single [PRODUCT] we make fixes these — here's how. [LINK IN BIO]
```

```
How to choose a [PRODUCT TYPE] in 30 seconds:

Step 1: [STEP]
Step 2: [STEP]
Step 3: [STEP]

No BS, no upsell. Just the checklist we give our own customers.
```

### Story captions (lifestyle, human)

```
This is what [OUTCOME] actually looks like on a Tuesday.

Not the highlight reel — just [PRODUCT] doing its job quietly in the background of a normal life.

That's the whole point of [BRAND].
```

```
I didn't expect [PRODUCT] to change my morning routine this much.

Now: coffee, [PRODUCT], done. The little things compound.

[SOFT CTA — e.g., "See it in action → link in bio"]
```

```
My customer sent this photo today. [DESCRIBE PHOTO]

She bought [PRODUCT] 3 weeks ago for [REASON]. This is what happened next.

Real people. Real results. That's [BRAND].
```

### Promo captions (direct response)

```
[OFFER] ends [WHEN].

[PRODUCT] — normally $[PRICE], today $[PRICE].

Here's why this price won't last: [REASON — limited stock, seasonal, cost increase].

→ [LINK]
```

```
The math on [PRODUCT]:

You spend $[X] on [ALTERNATIVE] every [TIMEFRAME].
[PRODUCT] costs $[Y] once.

That's $[Z] back in your pocket per year.

→ Grab yours before [WHEN]: [LINK]
```

```
Your cart called. It wants [PRODUCT].

But seriously — [NUMBER] people bought this week. When it's gone, it's gone (restock [WHEN], if at all).

→ [LINK]
```

### Question captions (comment magnets)

```
Quick question: what's the one thing you wish your [PRODUCT CATEGORY] did better?

I'll read every comment — and yes, several of your answers are already on our roadmap. 👀
```

```
Be honest: have you ever bought a [PRODUCT TYPE] that didn't work?

What went wrong? Tell me in the comments — the answers genuinely help us build better products.
```

```
This or that:

[A OPTION] or [B OPTION]?

Drop your answer below. 👇

(Wrong answers get a discount code. Kidding. Or am I?)
```

> **PRO TIP:** End every promo caption with a question disguised as urgency: "How many have you got left?" beats "Shop now" every time — it forces the prospect to engage instead of scroll.

## Chapter 2: TikTok & Reels Hooks for Ecommerce

The first 1–3 seconds decide everything. These hooks are grouped by structure. Pair any hook with your product demo in the next 5 seconds.

### Problem-agitate-solve (PAS)

```
"Your [PRODUCT CATEGORY] is broken and you don't even know it."
```
```
"Stop wasting money on [ALTERNATIVE]. Here's what actually works."
```
```
"I spent $[X] and [NUMBER] years figuring out [PROBLEM]. Here's the shortcut."
```
```
"Nobody talks about this [CATEGORY] mistake. It cost me [X]."
```
```
"If your [THING] still [BAD OUTCOME], you're doing it wrong."
```

### Demo hooks

```
"Watch what happens when I [ACTION] with [PRODUCT]."
```
```
"This is [PRODUCT] after [TIMEFRAME]. No filters."
```
```
"One spray of this and watch — [VISIBLE RESULT]."
```
```
"I didn't believe it either until I tried [PRODUCT]."
```
```
"POV: [PRODUCT] actually works like they say."
```

### Myth-bust hooks

```
"[COMMON BELIEF] about [CATEGORY]? Total myth."
```
```
"3 things 'experts' tell you about [CATEGORY] that are wrong."
```
```
"Expensive doesn't mean better. Here's proof."
```
```
"You don't need [COMMON SOLUTION]. You need this instead."
```

### Behind-the-scenes hooks

```
"This is how [PRODUCT] is actually made. You're not ready."
```
```
"A day in the warehouse that ships [NUMBER] orders."
```
```
"We tested [NUMBER] versions of this before this one."
```
```
"The packaging unboxing your customers actually film."
```

### Trend-jack hooks (swap [TREND] for whatever's on your FYP)

```
"[TREND] but make it [PRODUCT CATEGORY]."
```
```
"The [TREND] transition, but with [PRODUCT]."
```
```
"Things that look aesthetic but actually [PRODUCT-RELATED TRUTH]."
```
```
"Rating [TREND] with [PRODUCT]. It's a 10/10 because [REASON]."
```

### Story hooks (highest-converting for DTC)

```
"I almost didn't launch [PRODUCT]. Here's why I'm glad I did."
```
```
"A customer emailed me crying about [PRODUCT]. Here's what happened."
```
```
"I was broke when I started [BRAND]. [PRODUCT] is what changed it."
```
```
"My worst product decision became our best seller."
```

> **KEY INSIGHT:** Hooks with numbers ("3 mistakes," "in 30 seconds," "$47") get roughly 2x the watch-through of vague hooks. Specificity is the scroll-stopper.

## Chapter 4: Hashtag Strategy

### The 30/20/10 rule

Every caption gets hashtags in three tiers:

- **10 broad tags** — your category (#skincare, #homegoods). Discovery, low intent.
- **10 mid tags** — niche + audience (#cleanbeauty, #smallbusinessowner). Moderate reach.
- **10 niche tags** — hyper-specific (#veganmoisturizers, #shopifyfounder). Low reach, high intent — this is where buyers hide.

> **KEY INSIGHT:** Niche hashtags convert. A tag like #smallbatchskincare reaches 5,000 people who are exactly your customer; #skincare reaches 50 million people who are nobody in particular.

### Rules

1. Rotate 3 sets weekly so you don't look spammy to the algorithm.
2. Never reuse the exact same 30-tag block twice in a row.
3. Create ONE branded tag (#yourbrandname) and use it in every post — it builds a searchable archive.
4. Check a tag before using it: if the top posts aren't your niche, skip it.
5. On TikTok/Reels, 3–5 tags beat 30 — use the niche set only.

`hashtags.csv` contains six ready-made sets: ecommerce-general, fashion, home-decor, beauty, fitness, handmade.

## Chapter 5: Stories, Highlights & DM Replies

### Story series that always works

| Series | Frequency | Format |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| "This or that" poll | 2x/week | Two product options |
| "Ask me anything" box | Weekly | Answer in next Story |
| "Behind the scenes" clip | 3x/week | Raw footage, no polish |
| "Customer spotlight" | Weekly | Repost tagged customer |
| "New drop countdown" | Pre-launch | Sticker countdown |
| "Restock alert" | When relevant | Countdown sticker |

### Highlight structure (covers sell followers)

Create 5 highlights, in this order:

1. **Start here** — who you are, what you sell, why it exists (3–5 frames).
2. **Reviews** — screenshots of 5-star reviews.
3. **How it's made** — process footage.
4. **FAQ** — answer the top 5 objections as text-on-screen.
5. **Shop** — current products with prices.

> **PRO TIP:** The FAQ highlight quietly does sales work 24/7. Every "is this worth it?" comment gets answered once, permanently, for every future visitor.

### Pinned-post strategy

Pin three posts to your Instagram grid, in this order:

1. **The proof post** — best review or UGC. Cold visitors see social proof first.
2. **The offer post** — current promotion or best-seller. Converts the warm.
3. **The story post** — founder origin or brand mission. Humanizes the brand.

Rotate the offer pin every campaign. Keep the proof pin until a better review appears.

## Chapter 6: Metrics That Matter + Weekly Review

### Vanity vs. revenue metrics

| Vanity (ignore) | Revenue (watch) |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Follower count | Profile visits → website clicks |
| Likes | Saves per post |
| Impressions | Comments per post |
| Views | DM inquiries |
| Reach | Orders attributed to social |

> **KEY INSIGHT:** A post with 2,000 views and 40 saves is worth more than a post with 20,000 views and 5 likes. Saves signal "I need this later" — that's purchase intent. Likes signal nothing.

### The weekly review checklist

Every Sunday, spend 15 minutes:

- [ ] Which post got the most **saves** this week? Make 3 variations of it.
- [ ] Which post got the most **comments**? Reply to every one — unanswered comments kill reach.
- [ ] Which **story** got the most replies? Repeat that format.
- [ ] Did profile visits convert to link clicks? If not, your bio link or pinned post needs work.
- [ ] Which hashtag set performed best? Rotate it back in next week.
- [ ] Any DM saying "how do I buy?" Note the phrasing — it belongs in your FAQ highlight.
- [ ] Which day had the best engagement? Shift your posting time to match.

### Monthly deep-dive

- Compare this month's saves/comments/sends against last month's.
- Identify your top 3 posts of all time. Repurpose each into a new format (carousel → Reel, Reel → Story series).
- Check which caption category drove the most profile-to-site clicks. Double down on it next cycle.
