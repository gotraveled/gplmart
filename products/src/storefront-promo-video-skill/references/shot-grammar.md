# Shot grammar for promos

## The 5-beat arc

1. Hook (0-3s): boldest visual or brand statement
2. Range (3-8s): 2-3 products/collections, hard cuts
3. Proof/mood (8-14s): texture, context, "why us"
4. Offer/CTA (14-20s): promotion/differentiator, big text
5. Logo close: end card, clean resolve

## Pacing curve

Shot lengths rise through the film: ~1.5-2s early (energy), ~3-4s mid (proof), ~4-5s end (resolve).
A 20s promo typically = 7-9 shots inside one or two generations.

## Hard rules

- Every shot = different action AND different camera move
- Max ONE 360-orbit per film
- Text cards: <=6 words each, <=3 cards total
- No repeated push-ins, no repeated orbits, no identical framing twice
- Logo only in the finishing end card - never in generated footage
- If two shots could be swapped without anyone noticing, one of them is filler - cut it
