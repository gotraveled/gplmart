# Pricing optimizer - charge what the market will pay, protect your margin

Pricing is the highest-leverage decision in ecommerce. A 10% price increase on a 30% margin product
= 33% more profit - no extra sales needed. This skill builds pricing that maximizes profit,
not just revenue.

---

## Intake - one batch

1. Product + COGS (cost of goods) + current price + margin
2. Competitor prices (2-3 examples if known)
3. Product category + perceived value level
4. Sales velocity: fast-moving / slow / seasonal / new
5. Goal: increase margin / move slow stock / compete on price / premium positioning
6. Constraints: MAP policies, supplier minimums, psychological price points
7. Any bundles/kits/comparisons to consider

## The pricing framework

### Step 1: Know your numbers

```
COGS = product cost + packaging + shipping (if you pay it)
Margin = (Price - COGS) / Price x 100
Target margin: 40-60% for most ecommerce (adjust by category)
Break-even ROAS = 100 / margin %
Example: 50% margin → break-even ROAS = 2.0x
```

### Step 2: Position your price

| Position | Price range | Customer perception | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budget | Bottom 25% of market | "Cheap/low quality" | Volume play, entry products |
| Value | Middle 50% | "Good deal" | Most products - sweet spot |
| Premium | Top 25% | "Quality/luxury" | Differentiated products, strong brand |
| Ultra-premium | Top 10% | "Status/exclusive" | Limited editions, luxury |

### Step 3: Psychological pricing

- **Charm pricing**: ₹499 not ₹500, ₹1,299 not ₹1,300 (left-digit effect)
- **Anchoring**: show "was ₹1,999, now ₹1,499" (was price must be real)
- **Decoy pricing**: 3 options - basic, standard, premium - most pick middle
- **Per-unit framing**: "₹5/day" instead of "₹1,500" for expensive items
- **EMI display**: "₹250/month" makes ₹2,999 feel affordable

## Discount strategy

### When to discount

- **New product launch**: 10-20% off first week to get reviews/momentum
- **Slow mover**: 15-30% off to clear stock (but check if it's a pricing problem vs product problem)
- **Seasonal clearance**: end-of-season 30-50% off (real clearance, not fake)
- **Bundle incentive**: "Buy 2 get 10% off" to increase AOV
- **Loyalty reward**: exclusive discounts for repeat customers (not public)

### When NOT to discount

- **Evergreen discount** (>30 days running) = your real price is higher, customers wait for sales
- **Race to bottom**: if competitor drops price, don't automatically match - check your margin
- **Premium positioning**: discounting cheapens perceived value
- **New product without reviews**: discount won't fix trust problem, get reviews first

## Bundle pricing

### Bundle types

- **Pure bundle**: products only sold together (soap + loofah set)
- **Mixed bundle**: buy X get Y at discount (buy shirt get 20% off pants)
- **Quantity break**: buy 2 get 10% off, buy 3 get 20% off
- **Gift bundle**: buy product get free accessory (buy serum get free applicator)

### Bundle math

```
Bundle price should be 10-15% less than buying separately
Example: Product A ₹500 + Product B ₹300 = ₹800 separately
Bundle price: ₹699-719 (10-15% off)
Your margin on bundle: still healthy if COGS stays same
```

## Dynamic pricing triggers

Adjust prices based on:

- **Inventory level**: low stock → raise price or add urgency; high stock → discount to move
- **Competitor price**: if they drop, decide: match, beat, or hold (don't panic-match)
- **Seasonality**: demand peaks → hold or raise price; troughs → discount strategically
- **Conversion rate**: high conversion + low stock = raise price; low conversion + high stock = test lower price
- **Ad performance**: if ROAS is great, you can afford higher prices; if ROAS is tight, optimize product/pricing first

## Price testing (how to test without losing money)

### A/B test method
- Split traffic 50/50 between two prices for 2 weeks
- Measure conversion rate AND revenue per visitor (not just conversion)
- Winner = higher revenue per visitor, not just higher conversion

### Price increase test
- Raise price 10% for new visitors only (existing customers keep old price)
- Monitor conversion rate drop - if <5% drop, keep new price
- If >10% drop, revert or find middle ground

### Anchor test
- Show "was ₹X" price that's never been your real price (if legal in your market)
- Compare conversion with and without anchor
- Only use if "was" price is defensible (was real recently or comparable product)
