# Dynamic pricing - triggers and rules

## When to adjust prices

### Inventory-based
- **Low stock (<20% remaining)**: raise price 10-15% OR add urgency ("only X left")
- **High stock (>60% remaining, slow mover)**: discount 15-30% to move
- **Seasonal stock**: end-of-season clearance pricing

### Competitor-based
- **Competitor drops price**: don't auto-match - check your margin first
- **Competitor raises price**: opportunity to capture market share at your current price
- **New competitor enters**: monitor but don't panic - focus on your differentiation

### Demand-based
- **Conversion rate high + stock low**: raise price (demand exceeds supply)
- **Conversion rate low + stock high**: test lower price OR improve product page first
- **Seasonal demand peak**: hold or raise price (Diwali, wedding season, back-to-school)
- **Seasonal demand trough**: discount strategically to maintain cash flow

### Ad performance-based
- **ROAS >300%**: you can afford higher prices OR scale ad spend
- **ROAS 150-300%**: optimize product page, test small price increases
- **ROAS <150%**: fix product/pricing before scaling ads

## Dynamic pricing rules

### Rules that work
- **Raise prices on winners**: if product sells out consistently, test 10-15% price increase
- **Lower prices on losers**: if product sits 60+ days, discount or discontinue
- **Match competitor only if margin allows**: never sell below COGS + shipping
- **Time-based**: "weekend special" pricing creates urgency without permanent discount
- **Quantity-based**: bulk discounts for B2B/wholesale buyers

### Rules that don't work
- **Algorithmic pricing like Uber**: customers hate unpredictable prices, kills trust
- **Frequent small changes**: customers notice, feels manipulative
- **Penalizing loyal customers**: new customers get deals, repeat buyers pay full price = resentment
- **Fake urgency**: countdown timers that reset = trust destruction

## Implementation

### Manual (small catalog)
- Review prices monthly
- Adjust based on inventory, sales velocity, competitor moves
- Document price changes and results

### Semi-automated (medium catalog)
- Shopify price rules or discount apps
- Inventory-based triggers (low stock = raise price)
- Competitor price monitoring tools

### Automated (large catalog)
- Dynamic pricing software (competitor intelligence tools)
- AI-powered repricing (Amazon sellers use this heavily)
- Custom rules via API

## Price change communication

- **Raising prices**: don't announce (customers don't need to know)
- **Lowering prices**: announce via email/WhatsApp ("price drop on your favorites")
- **Sales**: clear start/end dates, real urgency
- **Grandfathering**: existing customers keep old price for X days (builds loyalty)
