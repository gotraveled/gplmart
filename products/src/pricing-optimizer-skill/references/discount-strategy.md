# Discount strategy - when and how

## Discount types

### Percentage off
- "20% off" - simple, universal
- Works best for mid-range products (₹500-2000)
- Avoid on already-cheap products (<₹300) - feels insignificant

### Fixed amount off
- "₹200 off" - better for expensive products (₹2000+)
- More concrete than percentage for high-ticket items

### Buy X get Y
- "Buy 2 get 1 free" - increases AOV, clears slow movers
- "Buy 1 get 1 50% off" - moves bundles

### Free shipping threshold
- "Free shipping over ₹999" - encourages higher cart value
- Set threshold 10-20% above average order value

### Gift with purchase
- "Free [accessory] on orders over ₹999" - adds perceived value without discounting product

## When to discount

### Good reasons
- **New product launch**: 10-20% off first 7-14 days to get reviews/momentum
- **Slow mover clearance**: 30-50% off to clear dead stock (but diagnose WHY it's slow first)
- **Seasonal clearance**: end-of-season real markdowns (Diwali collection after Diwali)
- **Bundle incentive**: "Buy 2 get 10% off" to increase AOV
- **Loyalty reward**: exclusive discounts for repeat customers (email/WhatsApp only)
- **Win-back**: "We miss you - 15% off your next order" for dormant customers

### Bad reasons
- **Matching competitor price**: they may have lower COGS or different strategy - don't panic-match
- **Evergreen discount**: always-on sales train customers to wait for discounts
- **New product without reviews**: discount won't fix trust problem - get reviews first
- **Race to bottom**: if everyone discounts, margins die for everyone

## Discount depth guide

| Situation | Discount depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| New product launch | 10-20% | 7-14 days |
| Slow mover clearance | 30-50% | Until sold out or 30 days |
| Seasonal sale | 20-40% | Sale period only |
| Bundle incentive | 10-15% | Always-on |
| Loyalty/VIP | 15-25% | Exclusive, not public |
| Win-back | 10-20% | One-time offer |

## Discount psychology

- **"Was/now" pricing**: only if "was" price is real and recent
- **Limited time**: real deadlines only - fake urgency destroys trust
- **Minimum spend**: "10% off orders over ₹999" increases AOV
- **First-order discount**: converts browsers to buyers, but don't make it permanent
- **Referral discount**: "Give ₹100, get ₹100" - costs less than ads

## Post-discount strategy

- **Raise prices back** after sale ends (don't keep discount price)
- **Email non-buyers**: "Sale ended - here's 5% off if you missed it"
- **Analyze what sold**: which products moved during discount? which didn't?
- **Don't discount bestsellers**: they sell at full price - discounting them loses margin unnecessarily
