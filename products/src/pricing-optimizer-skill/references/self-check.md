# Pricing audit checklist

## Before setting/changing prices

- [ ] COGS calculated accurately (product + packaging + shipping + payment fees)
- [ ] Target margin defined (40-60% for most ecommerce)
- [ ] Break-even ROAS calculated (100 / margin %)
- [ ] Competitor prices checked (2-3 examples)
- [ ] Market positioning decided (budget/value/premium)
- [ ] Psychological pricing applied (charm pricing, anchoring)

## Pricing structure

- [ ] Prices end in 9 or 99 (unless premium positioning)
- [ ] "Was" prices are real and recent (not fake anchors)
- [ ] EMI/per-unit framing used for expensive products
- [ ] Bundle options available (increase AOV)
- [ ] Quantity breaks for consumables
- [ ] Free shipping threshold set (10-20% above AOV)

## Discount strategy

- [ ] Discounts have clear start/end dates
- [ ] Discount depth appropriate for situation (10-20% launch, 30-50% clearance)
- [ ] No evergreen discounts (>30 days running)
- [ ] Bestsellers not discounted unnecessarily
- [ ] Loyalty/VIP discounts exclusive (not public)

## Margin protection

- [ ] Ad spend accounted for in pricing (ROAS must exceed break-even)
- [ ] Return rate factored into margin (30% returns = need higher margin)
- [ ] Payment gateway fees included in COGS
- [ ] Shipping costs covered (or free shipping threshold profitable)
- [ ] Platform fees included (Amazon/Flipkart commission)

## Monitoring

- [ ] Price changes documented (what, when, why, result)
- [ ] Margin tracked monthly (not just revenue)
- [ ] Slow movers identified (>60 days, <X units/month)
- [ ] Bestsellers identified (consider price increase test)
- [ ] Competitor prices monitored monthly

## Red flags

- [ ] Selling below COGS (loss leader without strategy)
- [ ] Margin <20% on most products (unsustainable)
- [ ] Discounting >50% regularly (margin death spiral)
- [ ] Prices unchanged for 6+ months (market moved, you didn't)
- [ ] No price testing ever (leaving money on table)
