# Ad copy - RSA headlines & descriptions

## RSA structure

Google rotates your headlines/descriptions to find best combinations. Give it options.

### Headlines (15 x 30 chars max)

Mix these types:
- **Keyword headline**: "Steel Lunch Box 3-Tier" (exact match to search)
- **Benefit headline**: "Keeps Food Hot 5 Hours"
- **Offer headline**: "Free Shipping Over ₹999"
- **Social proof**: "10,000+ Happy Customers"
- **USP**: "BPA-Free Food Grade Steel"
- **CTA**: "Shop Now - Best Price"
- **Urgency**: "Sale Ends Sunday" (only if real)

### Descriptions (4 x 90 chars max)

- **Feature + benefit**: "Triple-layer insulation keeps tiffin hot for 5+ hours. Perfect for office and school."
- **Trust + offer**: "Food-grade stainless steel, BPA-free. Free shipping on orders over ₹999. Easy 7-day returns."
- **Use case**: "Ideal for office lunch, school tiffin, gym meals. 3 compartments keep food separate."
- **Social proof + CTA**: "Join 10,000+ customers who switched. Shop now and get 10% off first order."

## Pinning strategy

Pin critical headlines:
- Position 1: keyword or benefit headline (most important)
- Position 2: offer or USP headline
- Leave others unpinned for Google to test

## Shopping ad "copy" = feed optimization

Shopping ads don't have traditional ad copy - your product feed IS the ad:

- **Title**: front-load keywords, include brand + product type + key spec
- **Description**: benefit-led, include use cases, materials
- **Image**: white background, high-res, product fills 85%+ of frame
- **Price**: competitive - Google shows your price next to competitors

## Ad copy rules for ecommerce

- Front-load keywords in headlines (search term matching matters)
- Include price/offer when competitive ("Under ₹500", "20% Off")
- Use numbers: "5-hour heat retention" beats "long-lasting"
- Add urgency only when real ("Sale ends Sunday" needs actual end date)
- Include trust signals: "Free shipping", "Easy returns", "COD available"
- Match ad copy to landing page - no bait-and-switch
