# Google Ads for ecommerce - capture high-intent buyers

Google Ads reaches buyers at the moment they're searching - highest intent traffic available.
This skill builds campaigns that profit from that intent: keyword strategy, ad copy, bidding,
shopping feeds and the optimization rhythm that keeps ROAS positive.

---

## Intake - one batch

1. Product/store + what you're selling + price range
2. Target geography (India / US / global)
3. Monthly ad budget (realistic number)
4. Goal: sales / leads / traffic / brand awareness
5. Current state: new to Google Ads / existing campaigns underperforming / scaling winners
6. Ecommerce platform: Shopify / marketplace / custom (affects feed setup)
7. Any constraints: margins, COD availability, shipping zones

## Campaign types for ecommerce (in priority order)

### 1. Shopping campaigns (highest ROI for product sales)

- Product feed → Google Merchant Center → Shopping ads
- Shows your product image + price + store name in search results
- Best for: visual products, price-competitive items, high purchase intent
- Setup: Merchant Center feed → Shopping campaign → target by product groups

### 2. Search campaigns (capture specific queries)

- Text ads on search results for specific keywords
- Best for: branded terms, competitor terms, specific product queries
- Structure: tightly themed ad groups (5-10 keywords each)

### 3. Performance Max (Google's AI-driven everything campaign)

- One campaign → all Google surfaces (Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail)
- Best for: stores with conversion tracking + enough data (>30 conversions/month)
- Requires: solid feed, conversion tracking, audience signals

### 4. YouTube ads (awareness + retargeting)

- Video ads before/during YouTube content
- Best for: product demos, brand awareness, retargeting website visitors
- Format: 6s bumper, 15-30s skippable, longer for consideration

## Keyword strategy

### Match types (modern approach)

- **Exact match** [keyword]: highest control, use for proven converters
- **Phrase match** "keyword": good balance, Google expanded this significantly
- **Broad match** keyword: only with smart bidding + conversion tracking, never manual CPC
- **Negative keywords**: critical - block irrelevant searches ("free", "DIY", "jobs", competitor brands you don't want)

### Keyword research sources (free)

1. **Google Keyword Planner**: search volume + competition (requires ads account)
2. **Google autocomplete**: real searches people type
3. **Google Shopping tab**: see what products/queries appear
4. **Competitor analysis**: what keywords do they bid on? (SpyFu, SEMrush free trials)
5. **Search terms report**: after launch, this is GOLD - actual queries triggering your ads

### Ecommerce keyword categories

- **Brand terms**: your store name (cheap, high conversion - protect these)
- **Product terms**: "buy [product] online", "[product] price india"
- **Category terms**: "kitchen storage containers", "office lunch box"
- **Competitor terms**: "[competitor brand] alternative" (legal but careful)
- **Problem-solution**: "how to keep food hot in tiffin", "best lunch box for office"

## Ad copy for ecommerce

### Responsive Search Ads (RSA) structure

- 15 headlines (30 chars each) - Google rotates combinations
- 4 descriptions (90 chars each)
- Include: product benefit, price/offer, USP, CTA, social proof
- Pin important headlines to position 1 or 2 for control

### Shopping ad optimization

- Product titles in feed = your ad copy (front-load keywords)
- High-quality images = higher CTR
- Competitive pricing = better ad rank
- Promotions/extensions: free shipping, sale badges, product ratings

## Bidding strategy

| Stage | Budget | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Launch (<30 conv/month) | Testing | Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks - gather data |
| Learning (30-100 conv/month) | Growing | Target CPA or Maximize Conversions |
| Scaling (>100 conv/month) | Profitable | Target ROAS or Maximize Conversion Value |

Never change bidding strategy mid-learning - Google needs 1-2 weeks stable.

## Budget allocation (India ecommerce benchmarks)

- Starting test budget: ₹500-2000/day for Shopping + Search
- Product launches: +50% budget for first week
- Scaling: +20-30% every 3-5 days when profitable
- Brand terms: small budget (5-10%) - defensive, not growth
- Non-brand: bulk of budget - this is where growth happens

## Optimization rhythm

**Daily** (5 min): check spend, conversions, disapproved ads
**Weekly** (30 min): search terms report → add negatives, adjust bids, refresh ad copy
**Monthly** (2 hours): full review - ROAS by campaign/product, budget reallocation, new keyword opportunities
