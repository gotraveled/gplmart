# Freelance Business Planner

Run your freelance business like a business: income, clients, taxes, pipeline and rates - all in one workbook with live formulas.

## What's inside
- **Freelance-Business-Planner.xlsx** - 6 tabs:
  - *Income Tracker* - every payment with Paid/Pending/Overdue totals
  - *Client CRM* - projects, lifetime value, follow-up dates
  - *Tax Set-Aside* - automatic 30% reserve calculation per payment
  - *Project Pipeline* - leads to paid with weighted expected revenue
  - *Rate Calculator* - your minimum hourly/daily/project rate from income goals
  - *Goals & Review* - monthly targets vs actual + Friday review ritual

## How to use
Open in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. Yellow cells are inputs; formulas do the math. Update weekly - the Friday review keeps it alive.
