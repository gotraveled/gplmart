## Why These Documents Matter

Launching a Shopify store without proper legal documents is like opening a physical store without a sign, a returns policy, or a way for customers to contact you. Here's what you're risking:

### Legal Protection

- **Privacy Policy:** Required by GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA (California), and India's DPDPA 2023 if you collect any customer data — which every Shopify store does
- **Terms of Service:** Protects you from liability, sets expectations, and gives you legal standing in disputes
- **Refund Policy:** Required by consumer protection laws in most countries — and by Shopify's own seller guidelines

Without these, you're exposed to chargebacks, legal claims, and platform penalties.

### Fewer Support Tickets

Every "Where is my order?", "Can I return this?", and "How do I pay?" question costs you time. Clear policies answer these before customers ask:

| Document | Questions It Answers |
|---|---|
| Shipping Policy | "How long does delivery take?", "Do you ship to my area?", "How do I track?" |
| Refund Policy | "Can I return this?", "How do I get a refund?", "What if it's damaged?" |
| FAQ Template | Everything else — sizing, payments, products, contact |

### Higher Trust & Conversion

New customers don't know your brand. Professional policies signal legitimacy:

- **68% of consumers** check return policies before buying (Baymard Institute)
- **Stores with clear policies** see higher conversion rates
- **Transparency builds trust** — especially for new or small brands

### Payment Processor Requirements

Payment gateways like **Razorpay, Stripe, and PayPal** often require:
- Privacy Policy (mandatory)
- Refund Policy (recommended)
- Terms of Service (recommended)

Missing these can delay or block your payment setup.

Open each file and search for `[` to find all fields you need to fill:

```bash
# In VS Code or any editor:
Ctrl+F → [
```

### Step 2: Replace Systematically

Work through each document top-to-bottom. Common replacements:

| Placeholder | Replace With |
|---|---|
| `[BRAND NAME]` | Your store name |
| `[EMAIL]` | Your support email |
| `[PHONE/WHATSAPP]` | Your contact number |

## Where to Place Each Document in Shopify

### Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, Shipping Policy, Terms of Service

**Option A: Shopify Policies (Recommended)**

1. Go to **Shopify admin > Settings > Policies**
2. You'll see sections for Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, Shipping Policy, Terms of Service
3. Paste your customized content into each section
4. Click **Save**

Shopify automatically displays these in:
- Checkout page (linked)
- Footer (if your theme supports it)
- Order confirmation emails (optional)

**Option B: Custom Pages**

If you prefer more control:

1. Go to **Online Store > Pages > Add page**
2. Title: "Privacy Policy" (or Refund Policy, etc.)
3. Paste your content
4. Save and publish
5. Add links in your theme's footer or navigation

### FAQ Template

**Option A: Dedicated FAQ Page**

1. Go to **Online Store > Pages > Add page**
2. Title: "FAQ" or "Frequently Asked Questions"
3. Paste your FAQs (format as Q&A or accordion)
4. Add to your navigation menu: **Online Store > Navigation > Main menu**

**Option B: Product Page FAQs**

Add relevant FAQs to individual product descriptions:

1. Go to **Products > [Your Product] > Description**
2. Paste relevant Q&As at the bottom
3. Format as: **Q: [Question]** / A: [Answer]

**Option C: WhatsApp Auto-Reply**

For quick responses:

1. Open **WhatsApp Business > Settings > Quick Replies**
2. Create replies for common questions (shipping time, payment methods, returns)
3. Use `/shipping`, `/payment`, `/returns` as shortcuts

### Email Templates

**Order Confirmation, Shipping, Delivery:**
- **Shopify admin > Settings > Notifications**
- Customize the default templates with your branding
- Or use **Klaviyo/Mailchimp** for advanced automation

**Welcome Series, Abandoned Cart, Review Request:**
- Requires email marketing platform
- **Klaviyo** (recommended for Shopify), **Mailchimp**, **Omnisend**
- Set up flows/automations with your templates

**Wholesale Inquiry Reply:**
- Save as a **canned response** in Gmail/Outlook
- Or create a template in your helpdesk (Zendesk, Freshdesk)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Copy-Pasting Without Customizing

**Problem:** Leaving `[BRAND NAME]`, `[EMAIL]`, or `[X]` placeholders in your live policies.

**Fix:** Search for `[` in every file before publishing. Every placeholder must be replaced.

### ❌ Mistake 2: Using Generic Templates

**Problem:** Shopify's default policies are generic and don't mention your specific products, shipping partners, or payment methods.

**Fix:** Our templates are Shopify-specific. Mention your actual couriers, payment gateways, and product types.

### ❌ Mistake 3: Hiding Policies in Footer

**Problem:** Customers can't find your policies, leading to support tickets and disputes.

**Fix:** Link policies prominently:
- Footer (all pages)
- Product pages (returns info)
- Checkout page (automatic in Shopify)
- FAQ page (cross-reference)

### ❌ Mistake 4: Inconsistent Information

**Problem:** Shipping Policy says "7-10 days" but FAQ says "3-5 days".

**Fix:** Use the same numbers everywhere. Create a "source of truth" document with your actual timelines.

### ❌ Mistake 5: Ignoring Legal Requirements

**Problem:** Missing required disclosures for your region.

**Fix:** Check local laws:
- **India:** DPDPA 2023, Consumer Protection Act 2019
- **EU/UK:** GDPR, Consumer Rights Directive
- **US:** CCPA (California), state consumer laws
- **Australia:** ACL, Privacy Act

### ❌ Mistake 6: Not Updating Policies

**Problem:** Policies say "Free shipping over ₹999" but you changed it to ₹1499.

**Fix:** Review and update policies quarterly or when business changes.

### ❌ Mistake 7: Forgetting Digital Products

**Problem:** Using physical product policies for digital products (templates, ebooks).

**Fix:** Digital products need different rules — instant delivery, no returns after download, license terms.
