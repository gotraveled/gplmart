## 2. Return Eligibility

### Eligible for Return/Exchange

- Items in **original condition**: unworn, unwashed, with tags attached, in original packaging
- Items with proof of purchase (order number or email confirmation)
- Items reported within **[X] days** of delivery (e.g., 7 or 14 days)

### Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned or exchanged:

- **Final sale items** marked "Final Sale" or "No Returns"

- **Earrings and items that come in direct contact with skin** unless defective
- **Gift cards**
- **Digital products** after delivery (see Section 1)
- **Personalized or made-to-order items**
- **Sale/clearance items** marked "Final Sale"

If your item arrives **damaged, defective, or incorrect**:

We inspect all claims and may request additional photos or video.
