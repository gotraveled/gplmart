## The 30-day launch plan

Use the tools in this order. Each week builds on the last.

### Week 1 - Foundation: know your numbers

1. **Open the Profit & Pricing System** and build your Product Costs sheet for your top 3-5 products.
2. Run each through the **Pricing Calculator** - find your break-even and target-margin price.
3. If you're selling on Etsy, open the **Etsy Toolkit** and price your first listing through the Fee Calculator. Log it in the Profit Tracker.

> **PRO TIP:** If your break-even price is above the market rate, don't launch yet. Either bundle products, cut costs, or find a niche where your price is justified. Pricing correctly on day one prevents the slow bleed of unprofitable sales.

### Week 2 - Marketing engine: copy and content

4. Open the **Ad Copy Swipe File** - pick 10 hooks from the angle that matches your product, swap the placeholders, and write your first ad or listing description.
5. Open the **Social Media Content Kit** - set up the 30-day calendar. Batch-write week 1's captions using the formulas.
6. Post consistently. The kit's calendar handles the planning - you just show up.

> **KEY INSIGHT:** Swipe files work because proven copy patterns repeat. The difference between a $0 product and a $10k product is often just better hooks - and you now have 200+ of them.

### Week 3 - Professional surface: documents and proposals

7. Fill in the **Shopify Store Launch Document Pack** - privacy policy, refund policy, shipping policy, FAQ. These protect you legally and increase buyer trust.
8. If you sell services or freelance alongside products, customize a **resume variant** and a **proposal template** from the Resume Kit.
9. Review your Etsy Toolkit **Listing Tracker** - which listings convert? Double down on those keywords.

### Week 4 - Review and scale

10. Run your first **monthly P&L** in the Profit System - know your real margin.
11. Check the **Etsy Dashboard** tab - revenue vs profit. If ad spend is eating margin, adjust.
12. Plan next month's content calendar and ad tests using the swipe file.

## How to resell these products (the empire part)

Every product in this bundle - like all GPLMart ebooks and digital products - includes **resell rights**. That means:

- You can list them on Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip or your own store **as your own products**
- You keep 100% of the sale price
- You can bundle them, rebrand them, or combine them with your own work
- You cannot claim authorship or resell the *membership* itself

### The high-margin play

Digital products have near-100% margins. A $17 spreadsheet costs you nothing to deliver - every sale is profit. The bundle strategy works because:

- **Single products** get discovered (SEO, Pinterest, Etsy search)
- **Bundles** get bought (higher perceived value, higher AOV)
- **Systems** get recommended (buyers tell other buyers)

### Product listing checklist (for reselling)

When you list any of these on your own store:

- [ ] Create mockup images showing the product in use (spreadsheet open on a laptop, planner on a desk)
- [ ] Write a benefit-driven title: outcome first, not features ("Know your real profit margin" not "Excel spreadsheet")
- [ ] Price at 2-3x what you paid attention to - digital products are priced on value, not cost
- [ ] Include 3-5 preview screenshots or sample pages
- [ ] Add a "what's included" list - buyers buy clarity
- [ ] Offer a bundle discount (buy 2+ products, save 20%)

> **PRO TIP:** The Resume & Freelance Proposal Kit and the Social Media Content Kit are your best standalone resell candidates - they solve emotional problems (career anxiety, content overwhelm) that buyers pay to escape.
