# Compliance - the rules that keep you legal

## Email (CAN-SPAM / GDPR / India DPDP)

- **Unsubscribe link**: every commercial email, works within 10 days, visible without login
- **Sender identity**: real business name + physical address in footer
- **Subject honesty**: no misleading subjects ("Re: your order" when it's not)
- **Consent**: only email people who opted in or have transactional relationship
- **GDPR (EU customers)**: explicit consent, right to deletion, data processing clarity
- **India DPDP Act**: consent-based, purpose-limited, breach notification

## WhatsApp Business API

- **Opt-in proof**: must be able to prove user gave permission (checkbox, verbal, form)
- **24-hour window**: free-form only within 24h of user's last message; outside = pre-approved templates
- **Template approval**: Meta reviews templates - write them clearly, avoid promotional language in utility templates
- **Quality rating**: too many blocks/reports = number gets restricted/banned
- **No purchased lists**: instant ban territory

## Universal

- Never scrape emails/phone numbers
- Honor unsubscribe/STOP within 24h (email) / immediately (WhatsApp)
- Keep consent records (who, when, how)
- Transactional emails (order updates) don't need marketing opt-in but DO need accurate sender info
