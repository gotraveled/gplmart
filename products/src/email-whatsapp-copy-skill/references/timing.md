# Send timing + frequency

## Email timing (best windows, IST)

| Day | Best time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | 10am-12pm | Highest open rates generally |
| Thursday | 2pm-4pm | Pre-weekend engagement |
| Sunday | 6pm-8pm | Leisure browsing, family time |

Avoid: Monday morning (inbox overflow), Friday evening (checked out mentally), late night.

## Frequency caps

- Promotional email: max 3-4/week for engaged subscribers, 1-2/week for cold lists
- Transactional (order updates): unlimited, always send
- WhatsApp: max 2-3 broadcasts/week - more gets you blocked

## Segmentation timing

- VIPs/repeat buyers: can handle more frequency + earlier access
- New subscribers: welcome series pace, don't overwhelm
- Win-back: space 7-14 days between touches
- Cart abandoners: time-sensitive, send within 1-48h regardless of "best time" rules

## Timezone logic

- India-focused store: IST send times above
- Global audience: segment by timezone or use "send at local time" feature (Klaviyo/Mailchimp support this)
- Never blast one timezone at 3am their time

## Frequency vs fatigue signals

Watch: open rate declining, unsubscribe spikes, spam complaints rising = you're sending too much or too irrelevant. Back off immediately.
