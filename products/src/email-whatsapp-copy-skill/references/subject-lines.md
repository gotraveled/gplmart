# Subject line formulas that get opens

## The anatomy

Subject = preview text partner. Together they answer: "why should I open THIS email?"

## Formulas (with examples)

**Curiosity gap**: "The one thing your [product] is missing"  
**Direct benefit**: "Get [result] in [timeframe]"  
**Question**: "Still [pain point]?"  
**Number/list**: "5 ways to [outcome]"  
**Personal**: "[Name], your [product] is back in stock"  
**Urgency (real)**: "Last 24h: [offer] ends tonight"  
**Social proof**: "10,000+ customers can't be wrong"  
**Behind-scenes**: "How we made [product] - inside look"  
**Pattern interrupt**: "Don't read this email" (works once, then burns)  

## Rules

- Under 50 chars (mobile truncates at ~40-50)
- No ALL CAPS, no excessive punctuation (!!!)
- No spam words: "FREE", "ACT NOW", "GUARANTEED", "$$$" - triggers filters
- Preview text continues the story, doesn't repeat the subject
- Emoji in subject: 1 max, only if brand voice allows - can boost or hurt, test it

## A/B test pairs to run

- Question vs statement: "Tired of X?" vs "X is finally solved"
- Number vs no number: "5 tips" vs "tips inside"
- Personal vs generic: "[Name], your order shipped" vs "Your order has shipped"
- Short vs long: "Sale ends tonight" vs "Our biggest sale ends tonight - 40% off"

## What kills open rates

- ALL CAPS subject
- Misleading subject ("You won!" when you didn't) - destroys trust + spam reports
- Same subject every send (people filter you out)
- No preview text (wastes half the real estate)
