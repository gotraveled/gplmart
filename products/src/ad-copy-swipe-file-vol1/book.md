## How to Use This Swipe File

A swipe file is a collection of proven copy you steal from. This one is organized so you can:

1. **Find your hook** — pick a category that matches what you're selling (problem, desire, or offer).
2. **Swap the bracketed tokens** — replace `[PRODUCT]`, `[AUDIENCE]`, `[OFFER]`, `[PRICE]`, `[TIMEFRAME]`, `[BRAND]` with your specifics.
3. **Test 3-5 variants** — never run one ad. Run five and let the market decide.
4. **Keep the winners** — when an ad hits, file it back into your own swipe file.

**Placeholder cheat sheet:**

| Token | Example |
|---|---|
| `[PRODUCT]` | "silicone kitchen tools" |
| `[AUDIENCE]` | "home cooks tired of flimsy utensils" |
| `[OFFER]` | "20% off + free shipping" |
| `[PRICE]` | "$29" |
| `[TIMEFRAME]` | "48 hours" |
| `[RESULT]` | "save 5 hours a week" |
| `[NUMBER]` | "10,000+ happy customers" |

> **KEY INSIGHT:** The best ad copy isn't clever — it's *specific*. "Get better results" is generic. "[AUDIENCE], get [RESULT] in [TIMEFRAME] without [PAIN]" is a hook. Always swap abstractions for specifics.

## Chapter 6: Power Words & Emotional Triggers

Use these to punch up any swipe line. One power word per hook — more feels salesy.

### 6.1 Power Words by Emotion

| Emotion | Power Words | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Curiosity** | secret, hidden, surprising, unexpected, nobody tells you, the truth about | "The hidden cost of cheap [PRODUCT]" |
| **Fear of Missing Out** | limited, exclusive, only, insider, secret, insiders | "Limited to [NUMBER] customers" |
| **Trust** | proven, guaranteed, certified, verified, trusted, award-winning | "Proven results in [TIMEFRAME]" |
| **Desire** | luxury, premium, elite, exclusive, rare, coveted | "The premium [PRODUCT] for [AUDIENCE]" |
| **Urgency** | now, today, tonight, instantly, immediately, fast | "Get [RESULT] today" |
| **Simplicity** | easy, simple, effortless, one-click, done-for-you | "Done-for-you [PRODUCT]" |
| **Authority** | expert, professional, certified, doctor-recommended, #1 | "#1 rated by [AUDIENCE]" |
| **Social Proof** | popular, trending, viral, everyone, [NUMBER]+, best-selling | "[NUMBER]+ [AUDIENCE] already switched" |
| **Value** | free, bonus, save, discount, deal, steal, bargain | "Free [BONUS] with every order" |
| **Transformation** | before/after, new you, upgrade, level up, breakthrough | "The breakthrough [AUDIENCE] have been waiting for" |

### 6.2 Emotional Triggers That Drive Clicks

| Trigger | What It Does | Example Hook |
|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | Agitates the problem | "Tired of [PAIN]?" |
| **Pleasure** | Promises the reward | "Imagine [POSITIVE STATE]" |
| **Fear** | Creates urgency through loss | "Don't let [NEGATIVE OUTCOME] happen" |
| **Belonging** | Taps into identity | "Join [NUMBER]+ [AUDIENCE] who [ACTION]" |
| **Status** | Appeals to aspiration | "The [ADJECTIVE] choice for [AUDIENCE]" |
| **Curiosity** | Opens an information gap | "What [AUDIENCE] don't know about [TOPIC]" |
| **Greed** | Maximizes perceived value | "Get [VALUE] for [PRICE]" |
| **Laziness** | Promises ease | "[RESULT] on autopilot" |

> **PRO TIP:** The best hooks combine two emotions. "Tired of [PAIN] and ready for [RESULT] without [EFFORT]" hits pain + laziness. Test single-emotion vs. dual-emotion hooks — dual usually wins.

| 1 | **Hook** | Determines if they stop scrolling | Pain hook vs. benefit hook vs. curiosity hook |
| 2 | **Offer** | Determines if they click | 20% off vs. free shipping vs. bundle deal |
| 3 | **CTA** | Determines if they convert | "Learn More" vs. "Shop Now" vs. "Get the Deal" |
| 4 | **Creative** | Determines if they engage | Video vs. image vs. carousel |
| 5 | **Headline** | Determines if they read | Benefit headline vs. urgency headline |

### 7.2 How Many Variants?

| Ad Budget | Recommended Variants | Testing Duration |
|---|---|---|
| $50-100/day | 3-5 | 3-5 days |
| $100-500/day | 5-10 | 5-7 days |
| $500+/day | 10-20 | 7-14 days |

### 7.3 How Long to Run?

- **Minimum:** 3 days or 1,000 impressions per variant (whichever comes first)
- **Ideal:** 5-7 days for statistically significant results
- **Don't kill early:** Let ads run long enough to gather data. Premature optimization kills winners.

### 7.4 What "Winning" Looks Like

| Metric | Good | Great | Exceptional |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CTR** | 1-2% | 2-4% | 4%+ |
| **CPC** | Below industry avg | 50% below avg | 75% below avg |
| **ROAS** | 2:1 | 3:1 | 5:1+ |
| **Conversion Rate** | 1-2% | 2-4% | 4%+ |

### 7.5 Testing Framework

1. **Pick one variable** — Don't change hook + offer + CTA at once. You'll never know what worked.
2. **Test 3-5 variants** — Enough to find a winner, not so many that budget is spread too thin.
3. **Run for minimum time** — Don't judge on day 1. Give it 3-5 days minimum.
4. **Kill losers, scale winners** — Reallocate budget to top 20% performers.
5. **Document everything** — Keep a swipe file of winners. Your future self will thank you.

> **KEY INSIGHT:** The 80/20 rule applies to ads: 20% of your creatives will drive 80% of your results. Your job is to find those 20% fast and scale them ruthlessly.

### Before You Launch

- [ ] Hook matches audience pain/desire/curiosity
- [ ] Body copy is specific (not generic)

- [ ] Check performance daily (but don't panic)
- [ ] Don't change more than one variable at a time
- [ ] Kill ads with <0.5% CTR after 3 days
- [ ] Scale ads with >2% CTR and positive ROAS
- [ ] Document winners and losers

### After the Test

- [ ] Identify the winning hook, offer, and CTA combination
- [ ] Scale budget on winners by 20-30% increments
- [ ] Create new variants based on winning elements
- [ ] File winning ads in your swipe file
- [ ] Plan next test cycle
