## 🔧 HABIT RETRO (answer at month end)

1. Which habit had the highest %? Why did it work?
2. Which habit had the lowest %? What broke it? (Too big? Bad cue? Life happened?)
3. What ONE adjustment for next month? (Shrink a habit, change a cue, or drop one.)
4. Should any habit be "graduated" — now automatic, remove from tracking?

---

## 💡 HABIT DESIGN RULES (cheat sheet)

- **Shrink until it's silly.** "Read 1 page" beats "read a book."
- **Cue stack:** attach new habit to an existing one ("after I pour coffee → journal").
- **Never miss twice.** One miss is an accident; two is a new pattern.
- **Track, don't judge.** A red day still counts as data.
- **Environment > willpower.** Phone in another room beats "I'll have discipline."
- **30-day trial.** No habit is evaluated before 30 days of honest tracking.
- **5 max.** If everything is a priority, nothing is.
