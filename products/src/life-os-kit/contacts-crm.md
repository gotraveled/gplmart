# LIFE OS — CONTACTS CRM

> Personal CRM. Not a sales tool — a relationship maintenance system. Goal: never lose touch accidentally.

**Updated:** [DATE]
**Review cadence:** Monthly (in [[weekly-review]] or standalone)

---

## 📇 HOW THIS WORKS

- Each person gets an entry with: context, last contact, next touch, notes.
- **Cadence tiers:** Inner circle (weekly/biweekly) · Friends (monthly) · Extended (quarterly) · Acquaintances (as needed).
- Monthly review: sort by "Days since contact" — anyone past their cadence gets a nudge.
- Keep it human: notes are for reminders, not surveillance. "Loves jazz, dog named Biscuit" beats "met at conference."

2. Who's overdue for a check-in per their cadence?
3. Any life events I should acknowledge? (New job, move, baby, loss)

5. Did I remember birthdays this month? (Check [[dashboard]] coming-up)

## 💡 PERSONAL CRM RULES

- **Quality over quantity.** 20 real relationships beat 200 contacts.
- **The 2-minute rule:** after meeting someone, add them immediately with one context line.
- **Notes decay gracefully.** Update "last contact" monthly — that's the system working.
- **Gifts > messages.** For inner circle, occasional physical gestures compound.
- **Reconnection script:** "Hey [name], been too long — how's [specific thing you remember]?" — specificity is the whole trick.
