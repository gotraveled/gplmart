---

## 📝 HOW TO USE THIS FILE

- **In Notion:** duplicate this page daily, or use a database with one page per day. Pin it to your sidebar.
- **In Obsidian:** one file per day in a `journal/` folder, or daily notes plugin (`Cmd+N` / `Ctrl+N`).
- **In Google Docs:** keep this as a running doc, insert the template at the top each day.
- **On paper:** print and keep a notebook — the structure carries over.

**The 2-minute rule:** if you only have 2 minutes, write "Top of mind" + "Today's focus" and stop. A stub entry beats a skipped day.

## 💭 PROMPT BANK (use when you don't know what to write)

**Morning prompts:**
- What would make today a win?
- What's the ONE thing that would make today feel successful even if everything else fails?
- What am I avoiding? (That's usually the important thing.)
- If I could only do one thing today, what is it?

**Evening prompts:**
- What gave me energy today? What drained me?
- Did today move me toward [[goals]]? Which goal?
- What would I do differently tomorrow?
- Who did I connect with today — and who should I reach out to?
- Am I proud of anything today, even small?

**Weekly/deep prompts:**
- What pattern do I see in the last 7 entries? (Mood, friction, recurring themes?)
- What did I learn about myself this week?
- What am I grateful for that I might have taken for granted?
- What would future-me thank me for doing differently?
- What story am I telling myself that might not be true?

**Gratitude micro-practice (optional, 1 line):**
- Today I'm grateful for: [EXAMPLE: The walk at lunch cleared my head]
