# Audit quality self-check

Before delivering the report:

- [ ] Every critical issue has evidence (URL/metric/screenshot description)
- [ ] Scores are justified - not arbitrary numbers
- [ ] Quick wins are actually quick (<30min, not "rebuild everything")
- [ ] Shopify fixes distinguish theme-level (free) vs app-level (costly)
- [ ] Content recommendations are specific ("write a 800-word guide to X") not vague ("create more content")
- [ ] Roadmap is realistic - no "rank #1 in a week" promises
- [ ] No black-hat tactics recommended anywhere
- [ ] Report is readable by a non-technical store owner
- [ ] Compared against at least one competitor where relevant
- [ ] Checked the obvious first - homepage title, mobile view, page speed
