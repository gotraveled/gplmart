## Writing rules

- Every issue needs EVIDENCE (URL, screenshot description, metric value)
- Effort ratings: S (<1hr) / M (half day) / L (multi-day)
- Impact ratings: Critical (blocking) / High / Medium / Low
- Priority = Impact x Effort - quick wins get flagged explicitly
