# Analysis quality checklist

## Before delivering

- [ ] Every claim has evidence (URL, price, screenshot description, metric)
- [ ] At least 3 competitors analyzed (1 is an anecdote, 3 is a pattern)
- [ ] Pricing is real numbers, not estimates
- [ ] Marketing analysis includes actual ad examples, not guesses
- [ ] Differentiation recommendations are specific ("niche to X" not "find your niche")
- [ ] "What to copy" items are patterns, not brand cloning
- [ ] Honest about your own weaknesses - no rose-colored analysis
- [ ] Roadmap is actionable ("add WhatsApp support" not "improve customer experience")

## Common analysis failures

- Listing features without explaining WHY they matter
- Comparing on features instead of outcomes
- Ignoring the customer's actual buying journey
- Recommending what big brands can do but you can't (yet)
- Forgetting the Indian market context (COD, WhatsApp, regional languages)
- Treating all competitors equally (your real competitor is the one taking YOUR customers)
