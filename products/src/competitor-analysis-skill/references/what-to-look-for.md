# What to look for - the signals that reveal strategy

## Supplier detection (dropshipping research)

- Product images with supplier watermarks or "AliExpress" style backgrounds
- Generic descriptions copied from supplier sites (same text across stores)
- No branded packaging in lifestyle shots
- Multiple unrelated categories = general dropshipper vs niche specialist
- Same product images across different stores = same supplier (CJ, AliExpress, Spocket)

## Positioning signals

- **Price point tells everything**: ₹199-499 = impulse/value, ₹999-2999 = mid-market, ₹3000+ = premium
- **Language reveals audience**: Hinglish = Indian mass market, pure English = urban/premium, regional = niche
- **Hero image style**: studio product shots = catalogue brand, lifestyle = brand experience, UGC-style = social-first
- **Headline formula**: "Shop [category]" = catalogue, "[Benefit] for [audience]" = positioned brand

## Marketing signals

- **Facebook Ad Library** (free): search brand name → see active ads, creative, how long they've run
- **Google search**: brand name + "review" = see if they're buying branded traffic
- **Instagram**: posting frequency, content type (product vs lifestyle vs UGC), engagement rate
- **YouTube**: product reviews mentioning them = influencer/affiliate activity
- **Email popup**: discount code offered = they understand list building

## UX/conversion signals

- **Countdown timers** = urgency marketing active
- **"Only X left"** = scarcity (real or fake - check if consistent)
- **Exit-intent popup** = they track abandoners
- **WhatsApp/chat widget** = Indian market focus
- **Multiple payment options** = they know COD/EMI matters in India
- **Reviews with photos** = they incentivize UGC reviews

## What their weaknesses look like

- Slow site (>3s load) = opportunity on speed
- No email capture = opportunity on retention
- Generic product descriptions = opportunity on copy quality
- Only Facebook ads, no Google/YouTube = channel gap
- No WhatsApp = India-specific opportunity
- Outdated theme/design = opportunity on perceived quality
