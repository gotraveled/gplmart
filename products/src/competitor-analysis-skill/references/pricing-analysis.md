# Pricing analysis - reading their strategy

## Price positioning bands (India ecommerce)

| Band | Range | Customer | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impulse | ₹99-499 | First-time, low-risk | Volume play, fast shipping, COD |
| Mid-market | ₹500-1999 | Value buyers | Bestsellers, bundles, reviews matter |
| Premium | ₹2000-4999 | Quality seekers | Brand story, warranty, unboxing experience |
| Luxury | ₹5000+ | Status buyers | Scarcity, exclusivity, influencer validation |

## Discount pattern analysis

Track over 2-4 weeks:
- **Evergreen discount** (always 20% off) = fake urgency, baseline is inflated
- **Real sale cycles** (Big Billion Days, Diwali, clearance) = genuine markdowns
- **Launch pricing** (new products at discount) = testing demand before full price
- **Bundle discounts** (buy 2 get 1) = increasing AOV
- **No discounts ever** = premium positioning or confident pricing

## Anchoring tactics

- Strikethrough "was" price - is the "was" price real? (check Wayback Machine)
- "Starting from ₹X" - hides the real entry price, check actual product prices
- Per-unit pricing ("₹5/day") - reframes expensive as affordable
- EMI display - makes ₹2999 feel like ₹250/month

## Shipping/returns as competitive weapon

- Free shipping threshold: where do they set it? (common: ₹499, ₹999)
- Express shipping cost: do they charge extra? by how much?
- Return window: 7 days vs 15 vs 30 - longer = confidence signal
- Return conditions: free returns vs customer pays = friction level

## The pricing question to answer

For each competitor: "Are they competing on PRICE, on VALUE, or on EXPERIENCE?"  
- Price competitors: you can't win on price alone - find another axis
- Value competitors: beat them on quality/proof/service
- Experience competitors: beat them on price or niche focus
