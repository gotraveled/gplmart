# Teardown template - per-competitor analysis

```
COMPETITOR: [name/URL]
ANALYSIS DATE: [date]

## Quick facts
- Store type: [Shopify/custom/marketplace]
- Catalogue size: [approx]
- Price range: [min-max]
- Geography: [India/global]
- Est. traffic: [SimilarWeb/visible signals]

## Product strategy
- Bestsellers (visible): [products]
- Niche focus or general store?
- Private label / reseller / hybrid?
- Supplier clues: [product images, descriptions, packaging shots]

## Positioning
- Homepage promise: "[exact headline]"
- Brand voice: [premium/value/playful/technical]
- Target customer: [who they're speaking to]
- USP claim: "[what they say makes them different]"

## Pricing & offers
- Entry price: [amount]
- Bestsellers price: [amount]
- Discounts: [sitewide %, seasonal, bundle]
- Shipping: [free threshold, flat rate, express]
- Returns: [window, conditions]
- Payment: [COD, EMI, wallets, BNPL]

## Marketing engine
- Facebook Ads: [active? ad count? creative style?] (check Ad Library)
- Google: [brand search ads? shopping?]
- Content: [blog posts/month, YouTube, Instagram]
- Email/WhatsApp: [popup offer, newsletter incentive]
- Influencer/UGC: [creators used, style, frequency]

## UX & conversion
- Homepage flow: [hero → categories → proof → offers?]
- Product page: [image count, video, reviews, urgency elements]
- Checkout: [guest?, payment options, upsell attempts]
- Trust: [testimonials, certifications, press, ratings]

## Verdict
- What they do BEST: [1-2 things]
- What they do WORST: [1-2 things]
- Copy-worthy pattern: [specific thing to adopt]
- Exploitable weakness: [specific gap to attack]
```
