# Competitor teardown - know exactly what you're up against

Stop guessing what competitors do. This skill produces a structured analysis of 1-5 competitor
stores: what they sell, how they position, what they charge, how they market - and where YOU can
win. Output = a teardown report + actionable roadmap, not a data dump.

Works on any public store URL. For Shopify stores, reads theme structure, collections, pricing,
and marketing patterns.

---

## Intake - one batch

1. Your store/product + what you're selling
2. Competitor URLs (1-5 stores to analyse)
3. Your goal: launch research / pricing check / differentiation / ad angle research / market sizing
4. Your current position: new entrant / established / testing new category
5. Geography: India / international / both
6. Depth: quick scan (homepage + top products) or deep dive (full catalogue + content + ads)

## The teardown framework (analyze each competitor)

### 1. Product & catalogue

- What they sell, price range, bestsellers (if visible)
- Catalogue size vs yours - are they broad or niche?
- Private label vs reselling? (check product descriptions for supplier clues)
- Product photography quality - studio shots vs supplier images vs AI?

### 2. Positioning & messaging

- Homepage headline + subhead - what promise are they making?
- Brand voice: premium / value / playful / technical
- Target audience signals (language, imagery, price points)
- Unique selling proposition - what do they claim that others don't?

### 3. Pricing & offers

- Price positioning vs market (budget/mid/premium)
- Discount patterns: sitewide sales, bundle offers, launch pricing
- Shipping/returns policy - free shipping threshold, return window
- Payment options: COD, EMI, wallets (India-specific)

### 4. Marketing engine

- Ad presence: check Facebook Ad Library, Google for their brand terms
- Content strategy: blog frequency, YouTube, Instagram activity
- Email/WhatsApp capture: popup offers, newsletter incentives
- Influencer/UGC: do they use creators? what style?

### 5. UX & conversion

- Homepage structure: hero, categories, social proof, offers
- Product page: images count, video, reviews, urgency elements
- Checkout friction: guest checkout, payment options, upsells
- Trust signals: testimonials, certifications, press mentions

## The "what to do" output

For each competitor, answer:

1. **What they do BETTER than you** - be honest, this is the point
2. **What you do BETTER than them** - your actual advantages
3. **What to COPY** - proven patterns worth adopting (UX, offers, content)
4. **What to BEAT** - their weakness you can exploit (price, niche, service, speed)
5. **The gap nobody owns** - the positioning nobody has claimed yet

## Rules

- Evidence over opinion: cite URLs, prices, screenshots-described patterns
- Don't just list features - explain WHY each pattern works or fails
- "Copy" means adopt the PATTERN, not clone the brand - trademark/identity stay yours
- Price analysis needs real numbers - don't estimate, fetch actual prices
- For dropshipping research: check if they use same suppliers (product images often reveal this)
