# Ecommerce Profit & Pricing System

Know your real margins before you spend a rupee on ads. A 3-tab Excel/Google Sheets workbook with live formulas - no spreadsheets skills needed.

## What's inside

- **Ecommerce-Profit-Pricing-System.xlsx** - the workbook:
  - *Start Here* - how the system works
  - *Product Costs* - cost sheet for every product (material, shipping, fees, ad cost) with landed cost, break-even and target price computed per row
  - *Pricing Calculator* - enter one product, get break-even price, target-margin price, ads-covered price and profit at market price
  - *P&L Dashboard* - 12-month log of revenue, orders, costs, fees and ad spend with gross/net margin calculated live

## How to use

1. Unzip and open the `.xlsx` in Excel, Google Sheets (File > Import) or Numbers.
2. Yellow cells are inputs - type your numbers, formulas do the rest.
3. Start with Product Costs for your top 10 sellers, then run each through Pricing Calculator before launching ads.

## Tips

- Price for profit, not for the market: if break-even is above the going rate, bundle, cut cost, or reposition.
- Update Product Costs whenever supplier prices change - stale costs lie.
- Log ad spend honestly in P&L; it's the difference between revenue and profit.
