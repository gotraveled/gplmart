# Subject lines + preview text

## Subject line formulas

**Order/transactional**: "Order confirmed - #[number]" / "Your order shipped!"  
**Curiosity**: "The one thing customers wish they knew"  
**Benefit**: "Get [result] in [timeframe]"  
**Question**: "Still [pain point]?"  
**Social proof**: "[X] customers can't be wrong"  
**Urgency (real only)**: "Last 24h: [offer] ends tonight"  
**Personal**: "[Name], your [product] is back"  
**Behind-scenes**: "How we made [product]"  
**Pattern interrupt**: "Don't read this (but you probably will)"

## Rules

- Under 50 chars (mobile truncates ~40-50)
- No ALL CAPS, no "FREE!!!", no spam trigger words
- Curiosity > clickbait - "Your order shipped" beats "You won't believe..."
- Personalize where possible (name, product, last purchase)
- A/B test: question vs statement, short vs long, emoji vs no emoji

## Preview text (the second subject)

Preview text appears next to subject in inbox - use it to continue the story:

- Subject: "Your order shipped!" → Preview: "Track your package and see delivery date"
- Subject: "We miss you" → Preview: "Here's 10% off + what's new since you left"
- Subject: "3 ways to get the most from your [product]" → Preview: "Quick tips most customers wish they knew"

Never repeat the subject in preview - wastes the space.

## A/B test pairs

| Test | Version A | Version B |
|---|---|---|
| Question vs statement | "Tired of X?" | "X is finally solved" |
| Number vs no number | "5 tips" | "tips inside" |
| Personal vs generic | "[Name], your order shipped" | "Your order has shipped" |
| Emoji vs no emoji | "Sale ends tonight 🎉" | "Sale ends tonight" |
| Short vs long | "Back in stock" | "Your favorite is back in stock" |
