# Segmentation - who gets which sequence

## Core segments

### By lifecycle stage

| Segment | Definition | Sequences |
|---|---|---|
| New visitor | Email captured, no purchase | Browse abandonment, welcome (if purchase) |
| First-time buyer | 1 purchase | Post-purchase, welcome series |
| Repeat buyer | 2-4 purchases | Cross-sell, loyalty, VIP early access |
| VIP | 5+ purchases OR high AOV | Exclusive offers, early access, personal touch |
| At-risk | No purchase 60-90 days | Win-back sequence |
| Lost | No purchase 180+ days | Sunset sequence, last-chance offer |

### By behavior

- **Browsers**: viewed products but didn't add to cart → browse abandonment
- **Cart abandoners**: added to cart, didn't purchase → cart recovery
- **Product interest**: viewed specific product multiple times → "still thinking about X?"
- **Price shoppers**: viewed sale/discount pages → deal alerts
- **Non-openers**: haven't opened in 60+ days → reduce frequency or sunset

### By product/category

- Kitchen buyers → kitchen accessories cross-sell
- Fashion buyers → styling tips, care guides
- Electronics → accessory recommendations, warranty info
- Consumables → reorder reminders (critical for repeat revenue)

## Segmentation rules

- **Never send same email to everyone** - segment by at least lifecycle stage
- **New customers get different messaging** than repeat buyers (don't assume they know you)
- **High-value customers get priority** - earlier access, better offers, personal touch
- **Engagement-based frequency**: openers get more, non-openers get less
- **Product affinity**: recommend based on what they bought/browsed, not random bestsellers

## Platform-specific segmentation

**Klaviyo**: predictive analytics (CLV, churn risk, next order date) - use these for smart segments  
**Mailchimp**: customer tags + groups - simpler but effective  
**Shopify Email**: customer segments based on order history - basic but works
