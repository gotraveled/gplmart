# Timing & frequency - when to send

## Best send windows (IST)

| Day | Best time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | 10am-12pm | Highest open rates |
| Thursday | 2pm-4pm | Pre-weekend engagement |
| Sunday | 6pm-8pm | Leisure browsing |

Avoid: Monday morning (inbox overflow), Friday evening (checked out), late night.

## Sequence timing rules

### Welcome
- Email 1: immediate (within 1 hour of signup/purchase)
- Email 2: day 2
- Email 3: day 4
- Email 4: day 7
- **Why**: front-load value while excitement is high

### Abandonment
- Email 1: 1 hour after abandon
- Email 2: 24 hours
- Email 3: 48 hours
- **Why**: time-sensitive, interest decays fast

### Post-purchase
- Email 1: day 1 (shipping confirmation)
- Email 2: day 7-10 (review request + tips)
- Email 3: day 21-30 (reorder/cross-sell)
- **Why**: spread out, don't overwhelm after purchase

### Win-back
- Email 1: when triggered (60-90 days inactive)
- Email 2: day 7
- Email 3: day 14
- **Why**: escalating urgency, space out touches

## Frequency caps

- **Engaged subscribers** (opened last 3 emails): max 4-5/week
- **Cold subscribers** (no opens in 30 days): max 1-2/week
- **New subscribers** (first 30 days): welcome pace only, no promos
- **VIP customers**: can handle more, but still max daily

## Fatigue signals (back off immediately)

- Open rate declining week-over-week
- Unsubscribe spike (>0.5% per send)
- Spam complaints rising
- Click rate dropping despite good subject lines

## Sunset policy

After 6 months of zero opens:
- Move to "sunset" segment (no more promos)
- Send one final "we'll miss you" email
- Remove non-openers from active list (protects sender reputation)
