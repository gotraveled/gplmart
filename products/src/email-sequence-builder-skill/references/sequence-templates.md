## Welcome series

### Email 1 - Order confirmation (immediate)

```
Subject: Order confirmed - [Order #12345]
Preview: Your [product] is being prepared. Here's what happens next.

Hi [Name],

Thanks for your order! 🎉

Your [product name] is being prepared and will ship within [X] business days.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:
✓ We pack your order carefully
✓ You'll get tracking details via WhatsApp/email
✓ Delivery in [X-Y] days

While you wait - here's a quick tip: [useful tip related to product]

Questions? Just reply to this email - we actually read them.

[Store name]
```

```
Preview: Still thinking it over? Here's a reminder.

Hi [Name],

You left [product name] in your cart - no pressure, just a friendly reminder.

[Product image]

Still want it? [Complete your order]

Questions about shipping, returns or anything else? Just reply - we respond fast.

[Store name]
```
