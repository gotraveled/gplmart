# Sequence audit checklist

## Before launch

- [ ] Every email has: subject, preview, body, CTA, footer
- [ ] Subject lines under 50 chars, no spam triggers
- [ ] Preview text continues story, doesn't repeat subject
- [ ] One email = one purpose, one clear CTA
- [ ] Timing follows sequence rules (not random)
- [ ] Segments defined (who gets what)
- [ ] Unsubscribe link present and working
- [ ] Sender info in footer (name, address)

## Content quality

- [ ] Hook works in preview (first 2 lines visible)
- [ ] Value-first approach (not just selling)
- [ ] Social proof included where relevant
- [ ] Objection handling in abandonment emails
- [ ] Real urgency only (no fake deadlines)
- [ ] Mobile-readable (short paragraphs, scannable)

## Technical

- [ ] Links tested (every URL works)
- [ ] Images optimized (not too heavy)
- [ ] Spam score checked (use mail-tester.com)
- [ ] Test sends to multiple email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail)
- [ ] Mobile preview checked

## Performance tracking

- [ ] Open rates tracked by sequence
- [ ] Click rates tracked by email
- [ ] Conversion attributed (which sequence drove sales?)
- [ ] Unsubscribe rate monitored (<0.5% per send)
- [ ] Spam complaints monitored (should be near zero)

## Optimization cadence

- [ ] A/B test subject lines monthly
- [ ] Review sequence performance quarterly
- [ ] Update copy for seasonal relevance
- [ ] Sunset inactive subscribers (protect sender reputation)
