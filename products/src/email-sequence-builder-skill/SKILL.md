# Email sequence builder - lifecycle flows that print revenue

Most stores send one-off promotional blasts. This skill builds the SYSTEM: automated sequences
that trigger on customer behavior - welcome, purchase, browse, abandon, win-back, VIP. Each
sequence is a mini-funnel with timing, copy and goals.

Output = complete email copy ready for Klaviyo/Mailchimp/Shopify Email, not strategy advice.

---

## Intake - one batch

1. Store/product + what you sell + average order value
2. Email platform: Klaviyo / Mailchimp / Shopify Email / other
3. Current state: no sequences / some exist but underperforming / starting from scratch
4. Customer lifecycle stage focus: new customers / repeat buyers / at-risk / VIP
5. Product type: consumable (reorder) / durable (repurchase rare) / fashion (seasonal)
6. Any offers to include: discount codes, free shipping thresholds, bundles
7. Brand voice: friendly / premium / playful / urgent

## The lifecycle map (which sequences matter)

```
NEW VISITOR → Browse Abandonment (if email captured)
     ↓
FIRST PURCHASE → Welcome Series + Post-Purchase
     ↓
REPEAT BUYER → VIP/loyalty + cross-sell
     ↓
AT-RISK (60-90 days inactive) → Win-Back
     ↓
LOST (180+ days) → Last-chance / sunset
```

## Core sequences to build

### 1. Welcome series (3-4 emails, 7-10 days)

| Email | Timing | Goal | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Immediate | Deliver + delight | Order confirmation + what happens next + brand story |
| 2 | Day 2 | Value-first | How to use product + quick tip/guide - no selling |
| 3 | Day 4 | Social proof | Reviews/testimonials + soft CTA to explore |
| 4 | Day 7 | Soft offer | First-purchase discount or bundle suggestion |

### 2. Post-purchase (3 emails, 14-30 days)

| Email | Timing | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1 | Order shipped/delivered confirmation + tracking |
| 2 | Day 7-10 | "How's it going?" + review request + care tips |
| 3 | Day 21-30 | Reorder reminder (consumables) or cross-sell (durables) |

### 3. Browse/cart abandonment (3 emails, 48h)

| Email | Timing | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 hour | Reminder + product image + "still thinking?" |
| 2 | 24 hours | Objection handler - FAQ, shipping, returns, guarantee |
| 3 | 48 hours | Final reminder + real urgency ONLY if true |

### 4. Win-back (3 emails, 30 days)

| Email | Timing | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 of trigger | "We miss you" + what's new + incentive |
| 2 | Day 7 | Different angle - bestsellers, new arrivals |
| 3 | Day 14 | Last chance - bigger incentive or sunset warning |

### 5. VIP/loyalty (ongoing)

- Early access to sales/new products
- Exclusive offers not available to regular customers
- Birthday/anniversary emails
- Referral program invites

### 6. Seasonal/campaign (as needed)

- Diwali, Eid, Republic Day, sale events
- Product-specific launches
- Clearance/stock clearance

## Email copy structure (every email)

```
SUBJECT: [under 50 chars, curiosity/benefit/urgency]
PREVIEW: [continues the story, doesn't repeat subject]

BODY:
[Hook - 1-2 lines, visible in preview]
[Value/content - the reason for this email]
[Social proof or objection handler]
[CTA - ONE clear action]

FOOTER: unsubscribe link, sender info
```

## Timing & frequency rules

- **Welcome**: immediate → day 2 → day 4 → day 7 (front-loaded)
- **Abandonment**: 1hr → 24hr → 48hr (time-sensitive)
- **Post-purchase**: day 1 → day 7-10 → day 21-30 (spread out)
- **Win-back**: day 0 → day 7 → day 14 (escalating)
- **Max frequency**: 3-4 promotional emails/week for engaged, 1-2/week for cold

## Segmentation rules

- New vs repeat buyers get different sequences
- High-value customers get VIP treatment earlier
- Browsers who didn't buy ≠ cart abandoners (different intent)
- Product category interest → relevant cross-sell recommendations
- Engagement-based: non-openers get fewer emails, openers get more
