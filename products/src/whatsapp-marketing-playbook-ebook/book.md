# The WhatsApp Marketing Playbook for Indian Ecommerce

**Turn WhatsApp from a support channel into your highest-ROI sales channel - broadcasts, catalogues, abandoned cart recovery and retention flows that actually convert.**

WhatsApp isn't just chat. For Indian ecommerce it's the channel where customers actually respond - 98% open rates vs 20% for email. But most stores use it wrong: blasting offers to everyone, getting blocked, wondering why it stopped working.

This playbook fixes that.

---

## Chapter 1 - Why WhatsApp converts (and why most stores fail at it)

### The numbers

- **98% message open rate** - vs ~20% email, ~5% SMS
- **45-60% response rate** on well-targeted broadcasts - vs 1-3% email CTR
- **Average order value 2-3x higher** on WhatsApp-assisted purchases
- **India has 500M+ WhatsApp users** - your customers are already there

### Why stores fail

1. **No opt-in strategy** - they message people who never gave permission, get blocked, blame WhatsApp
2. **Wall-of-text broadcasts** - 3-paragraph offers nobody reads
3. **No segmentation** - same message to new buyers and 2-year customers
4. **Wrong frequency** - daily blasts = instant mute/block
5. **No catalogue integration** - making customers ask "price?" instead of showing the product

### The golden rule

WhatsApp is a CONVERSATION channel, not a broadcast channel. Treat it like texting a friend who might buy from you - short, personal, relevant, timely.

## Chapter 2 - The foundation: opt-in, API and the rules

### Getting permission (the only legal way)

You need explicit opt-in before messaging anyone commercially:

- **Checkout opt-in**: checkbox at purchase ("Get order updates & offers on WhatsApp")
- **Website popup**: exit-intent or scroll-triggered WhatsApp opt-in
- **WhatsApp Business QR**: "Chat with us" button on site/product pages
- **In-store/packaging**: "WhatsApp us for deals" on packaging inserts

Store proof: timestamp, phone number, source (checkout/popup/QR), exact opt-in text. WhatsApp can ask for it.

### WhatsApp Business API vs regular WhatsApp

- **Regular WhatsApp Business app**: fine for <50 conversations/day, manual replies, small catalogues
- **WhatsApp Business API** (via Twilio/Interakt/WATI/AiSensy): required for broadcasts >100, automated sequences, catalogue integration, team inbox

For any real store: API is mandatory. Personal WhatsApp gets banned for bulk messaging.

### The 24-hour rule

- Within 24h of customer's last message: you can send ANY free-form message
- Outside 24h: only pre-approved TEMPLATE messages (Meta reviews them)
- Templates: order updates, shipping alerts, abandoned cart, promotional - each needs approval

### What gets you banned

- Messaging non-opted-in numbers
- Too many blocks/reports (quality rating drops)
- Using personal WhatsApp for bulk
- Sending prohibited content (gambling, adult, misleading claims)

## Chapter 3 - The broadcast that actually works

### Anatomy of a high-converting broadcast

```
Hi [Name]! 👋

[Store name] here - [one-line context: "we just restocked" / "our biggest sale"]

[Product/hook - ONE thing, not a list]
[Offer - clear, single]
[Link]

Questions? Just reply - real humans here.
```

### Rules

- **Under 500 characters** - longer gets skimmed or ignored
- **One product or one offer per broadcast** - never a catalogue dump
- **Personal greeting** - "Hi [Name]" not "Dear customer"
- **Emoji 1-2 max** - 📦 for shipping, 🔥 for sale, not 🎉🎊✨🎁🎈
- **Link at the end** - never mid-message
- **Reply prompt** - "Questions? Reply here" doubles as engagement signal

### Broadcast types that convert

**New arrival**: product hero + "just landed" + early access for subscribers  
**Restock alert**: "Back in stock" + social proof ("sold out twice") + urgency if real  
**Sale/offer**: discount code + deadline (only if real) + bestsellers  
**Content/value**: "3 ways to use [product]" + soft product mention - builds relationship between sales pushes

## Chapter 4 - The automated flows (where the real money is)

Broadcasts are one-time. Flows run forever. These are the sequences every store needs:

### Order confirmation + shipping updates

```
Day 0: "Order confirmed! #[number]. You'll get tracking shortly."
Day 1-3: "Your order shipped! Track: [link]"
Day 5-7: "Out for delivery today!"
Day 7+: "Delivered! How's [product]? Reply with feedback."
```

These aren't marketing - they're expected service. WhatsApp open rates mean customers actually see them.

### Abandoned cart recovery

| Timing | Message | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 hour | "You left [product] in your cart - still want it? [link]" | Gentle reminder |
| 24 hours | "Quick question - was it the price, shipping, or something else? Happy to help." | Objection handler |
| 48 hours | "Last reminder - [product] is almost gone. [link]" | Urgency (only if stock is real) |

Cart recovery via WhatsApp converts 3-5x better than email because it's a conversation, not a blast.

### Post-purchase / review request

```
Day 7 after delivery: "Hey [Name]! How's your [product] working out?
If you have 30 seconds, a quick review helps other buyers:
[review link]

Or just reply here and tell me - I read every message."
```

### Win-back (90+ days inactive)

```
"Hi [Name]! It's been a while - we've added some new stuff you might like.
[Best sellers or new arrivals]

What brings you back? [link]"
```

## Chapter 5 - Catalogue & product sharing

### WhatsApp catalogue setup

- Add products via WhatsApp Business app or API catalogue
- Each product: name, price, image, description, link to store
- Customers browse IN WhatsApp - no need to leave to see products

### Sharing products in chat

When customer asks about a product:

1. Send the catalogue item (not just a link - the actual product card)
2. Follow with one qualifying question: "What size are you looking for?" or "Is this a gift?"
3. Answer their question, THEN send the buy link

This feels like a salesperson, not a bot - and converts because it answers the real question.

### Broadcast-to-catalogue flow

```
Broadcast: "New arrivals just dropped - check these out"
→ Send 3-4 catalogue product cards (not links, actual product cards)
→ "Which one caught your eye? Reply with the number."
→ Customer replies → you send buy link for that product
```

The reply opens a 24h window for unlimited follow-up.

## Chapter 6 - Segmentation: the difference between spam and sales

### Segments that matter

| Segment | Who | What to send |
|---|---|---|
| New buyers | First purchase <30 days | Welcome series, product tips, review request |
| Repeat buyers | 2+ purchases | Loyalty offers, new arrivals, VIP early access |
| Cart abandoners | Added to cart, didn't buy | Recovery sequence (above) |
| Dormant | No purchase 90+ days | Win-back sequence |
| High-value | Top 10% spenders | Exclusive offers, personal check-ins |
| Product interest | Browsed but didn't buy | "Still thinking about [product]?" |

### Frequency rules

- **New buyers**: max 2-3 messages/week
- **Repeat buyers**: max 3-4/week (they opted in more recently)
- **Win-back**: max 1/week until they re-engage or opt out
- **Never message the same segment twice in 24h** - that's when people block you

## Chapter 7 - Compliance & keeping your number safe

### India-specific rules

- **DPDP Act**: consent-based messaging, data minimization, breach notification
- **TRAI DND**: promotional messages to DND-registered numbers can attract penalties
- **WhatsApp's own rules**: opt-in proof, template approval, quality rating

### Quality rating protection

WhatsApp tracks: blocks, reports, mutes per number. If quality drops:

- Messaging frequency too high → reduce
- Irrelevant messages → improve segmentation
- Too many non-opted-in → clean your list
- Quality rating tiers: Green → Yellow → Red → restricted/banned

### The unsubscribe that works

Every broadcast should include opt-out path:

- "Reply STOP to opt out" (manual but works for small lists)
- API tools auto-handle opt-out keywords
- Honor within 24h, remove from ALL future broadcasts immediately

One ignored STOP = one more report = quality rating drops.

## Chapter 8 - Measuring what works

### Metrics that matter

| Metric | Good | Great | Action if low |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery rate | >95% | >98% | Clean list, check opt-in quality |
| Read rate | >80% | >90% | Shorter messages, better timing |
| Reply rate | >5% | >10% | More conversational, ask questions |
| Click rate | >10% | >20% | Better link placement, stronger CTA |
| Conversion rate | >2% | >5% | Better offer, right segment |

### What to A/B test

- Message length: short vs detailed
- Emoji use: with vs without
- CTA placement: link mid-message vs end
- Timing: morning vs evening broadcasts
- Personalization: "Hi [Name]" vs "Hey!"

### The weekly rhythm

- **Mon**: send broadcast to engaged segment (new arrivals/offers)
- **Wed**: check replies, answer questions, send catalogue items to interested customers
- **Fri**: win-back or re-engagement to dormant segment
- **Sun**: review metrics, plan next week's broadcasts
